NHL Starting Goalies, Friday, October 30, 2026

Games on Friday, October 30, 2026. Last checked 2026-10-31 15:45 UTC. Statuses: Confirmed = team, league or on-site reporter says he starts. Expected = credible projection, not official. Unconfirmed = no reliable word yet.

New Jersey Devils at Vegas Golden Knights 6 p.m. ET

  • Devils: Connor Hellebuyck Confirmed
    Started and made 19 saves on 21 shots in a 5-2 win, his second straight victory since New Jersey acquired him from Winnipeg on Oct. 23, per the NHL.com box score and recap. Source: NHL.com GameCenter (Devils at Golden Knights, Oct. 30).
  • Golden Knights: Adin Hill Confirmed
    Started and made 27 saves on 31 shots in 58:12 of a 5-2 loss, per the NHL.com box score. Vegas is 1-3-1 in its past five, NHL.com says. Source: NHL.com GameCenter (Devils at Golden Knights, Oct. 30).

Winnipeg Jets at Florida Panthers 7 p.m. ET

  • Jets: Nico Daws Confirmed
    Made his first start for Winnipeg and stopped 33 of 37 shots in 58:06 of a 5-2 loss, the Jets' eighth straight defeat (0-6-2), per the NHL.com box score and recap. Source: NHL.com GameCenter (Jets at Panthers, Oct. 30).

    Made 22 saves on 24 shots in a 5-2 win, his third straight win and fifth in his past six starts, per the NHL.com box score and recap. Coach Paul Maurice had named him the starter on game day. Source: NHL.com GameCenter (Jets at Panthers, Oct. 30).
