NHL Starting Goalies, Tuesday, October 27, 2026

Games on Tuesday, October 27, 2026. Last checked 2026-10-27 21:45 UTC. Statuses: Confirmed = team, league or on-site reporter says he starts. Expected = credible projection, not official. Unconfirmed = no reliable word yet.

Columbus Blue Jackets at Philadelphia Flyers 7 p.m. ET

  • Blue Jackets: Cam Talbot Confirmed
    Columbus reporter Dave Maetzold, relaying coach Rick Bowness's morning update, reported that Talbot gets a second straight start after Saturday's game against Vegas. NHL.com's projected lineup also lists him first. Source: Dave Maetzold on X (Oct. 27).
  • Flyers: Dan Vladar Confirmed
    Coach Rick Tocchet said after the morning skate that Vladar starts, as PHLY Sports' Charlie O'Connor and the Inquirer's Jackie Spiegel reported. Joseph Woll started Saturday against the Ducks. Source: Charlie O'Connor, PHLY Sports, on X (Oct. 27).

Boston Bruins at Carolina Hurricanes 8 p.m. ET

  • Bruins: Jeremy Swayman Expected
    NESN's Adam Pellerin posted "Jeremy Swayman in net" on Tuesday. That is a reporter's lineup note rather than a coach's quote, so he is listed as expected. Swayman also started Saturday against the Sharks. Source: Adam Pellerin, NESN, on X (Oct. 27).
  • Hurricanes: Pyotr Kochetkov Expected
    Kochetkov was in the starter's crease at Carolina's morning skate, with Brandon Bussi in the backup spot, and NHL.com's projected lineup lists him first. Not a coach's announcement, so listed as expected. He also started Saturday's 4-0 win at St. Louis. Source: Walt Ruff, Hurricanes, on X (Oct. 27).

Los Angeles Kings at Chicago Blackhawks 8 p.m. ET

  • Kings: Anton Forsberg Expected
    Second night of a back-to-back. The Kings did not hold a morning skate; NHL.com says Forsberg is expected to start after Darcy Kuemper made 23 saves in Monday's win at the Rangers. Source: NHL.com (Kings at Blackhawks projected lineups).
  • Blackhawks: Spencer Knight Expected
    NHL.com says Knight is expected to make his seventh straight start, and Charlie Roumeliotis reported he worked in the starter's net at the morning skate. Not a coach's announcement, so listed as expected. Source: NHL.com (Kings at Blackhawks projected lineups).

Montreal Canadiens at St. Louis Blues 8 p.m. ET

  • Canadiens: Jacob Fowler Confirmed
    The Canadiens' official X account posted that Fowler starts. NHL.com's earlier projection had listed Jakub Dobes first; the team announcement takes precedence. Fowler also started Sunday at the Jets. Source: Montreal Canadiens on X (Oct. 27).
  • Blues: Jordan Binnington Confirmed
    NHL.com's game-day status report: "Binnington starts for the first time since Oct. 17." He was also in the starter's net at the morning skate, the Post-Dispatch's Matthew DeFranks reported. Joel Hofer started Saturday. Source: NHL.com (Canadiens at Blues projected lineups).
