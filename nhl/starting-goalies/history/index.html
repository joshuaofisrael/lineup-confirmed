Who started in goal? NHL starting goalie history, with a source on every row

Confirmed NHL starting goalies from this desk's nightly board. Every row links to an NHL.com or club page, and the same file is a CSV.

Last updated November 21, 2026. The file is rebuilt from the nightly board.

This file lists 84 confirmed NHL starts from the nights on our starting goalie board. The board runs from October 25, 2026 through November 2, 2026. Rows in the file run from October 25, 2026 through October 31, 2026, which is every confirmed start in that span whose cited source is an NHL.com page or an NHL club page. Download the same rows as CSV.

A row gets in when the board's status is confirmed and the source URL is on nhl.com or nhle.com, including team sites on an nhl.com subdomain and the league's box-score feed. 11 confirmed board entries stay out because the source is a reporter's post or another site. 11 entries are still marked expected, and 4 are unconfirmed. Those stay on the dated boards. The labels are the ones in how a starter gets confirmed.

November 1 and November 2, 2026 are on the board and have no row here. November 2 is still a set of projections. Why save totals are not a column

Save and shot totals stay on the linked source. Copying them into a spreadsheet froze at least one wrong pair.

On Nov. 21, 2026 the box-score feed for game 2026020200 (Flames at Oilers, Oct. 28) listed T. Jarry at 24 saves on 24 shots in 60:00, D. Cooley at 8 saves on 9 shots, and D. Wolf at 20 saves on 23 shots. That matches the notes already on the Oct. 28 board. The same day's check of game 2026020220 (Oilers at Islanders, Oct. 31) listed T. Jarry at 18 saves on 18 shots and I. Sorokin at 21 saves on 24 shots, time on ice 59:26, in a 4-0 Oilers win. The board had said Sorokin made 20 saves on 23 shots. That note now quotes the feed, and it keeps the earlier figure so the change is visible. The Oct. 31 board is the place to read it.

A preview, a recap, or a status report can be enough to confirm who started. It is a poor place to copy a save total from. The morning skate explainer is where a projection stays expected until a team, the league, or the box score says otherwise.
