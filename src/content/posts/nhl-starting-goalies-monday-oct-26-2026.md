**Monday evening update (5 p.m. ET).** Five of the six starters are now confirmed:

- **Rangers: James Reimer, confirmed.** [NHL.com's projected lineups](https://www.nhl.com/news/los-angeles-kings-new-york-rangers-game-preview-october-26-2026) say the 38-year-old will make his first start for New York, a day after signing. Dylan Garand is the backup.
- **Kings: Darcy Kuemper, expected.** [LA Kings Insider](https://lakingsinsider.com/2026/10/26/10-26-preview-power-play-changes-possible-zuccarello-out-upper-body-byfield-preparing-to-play-ward-expected/) says to look for Kuemper back in net. We have not seen a team announcement.
- **Maple Leafs: Sergei Bobrovsky, confirmed**, and **Flames: Devin Cooley, confirmed** for a fourth straight start, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/toronto-maple-leafs-calgary-flames-game-preview-october-26-2026).
- **Red Wings: Michal Postava, confirmed** for his first NHL start, announced by coach Todd McLellan ([MLive](https://www.mlive.com/redwings/2026/10/michal-postava-faces-tough-test-in-first-nhl-start-for-red-wings.html)). More in our [Postava story](/articles/red-wings-michal-postava-first-nhl-start-oilers-oct-26-2026/).
- **Oilers: Tristan Jarry, confirmed** by the team [on X](https://x.com/EdmontonOilers/status/2114773427089523157).

The original preview from before the announcements follows.

Monday, Oct. 26 brings three games on the [NHL schedule](https://www.nhl.com/schedule/2026-10-26), and as of Sunday afternoon no team has named its starter. All six teams played on Saturday, so nobody is on the second night of a back-to-back. Below is who started each club's most recent game, taken from the official NHL box scores. We will mark starters confirmed on the [Monday board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-26/) as teams announce them.

## Los Angeles Kings at New York Rangers, 7 p.m. ET

**Kings: unconfirmed.** Anton Forsberg started Saturday's 3-1 loss at New Jersey and made 23 saves on 25 shots, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/lak-vs-njd/2026/10/24/2026020169). Darcy Kuemper started the game before that, Thursday at the Islanders.

**Rangers: unconfirmed.** Dylan Garand started Saturday's 7-4 loss at Ottawa, stopping 17 of 22 shots, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/nyr-vs-ott/2026/10/24/2026020171).

## Toronto Maple Leafs at Calgary Flames, 8:30 p.m. ET

**Maple Leafs: unconfirmed.** Anthony Stolarz started Saturday's 7-2 win at Edmonton with 30 saves on 32 shots, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/tor-vs-edm/2026/10/24/2026020176).

**Flames: unconfirmed.** Devin Cooley started Saturday's 4-1 win over Detroit and made 28 saves on 29 shots, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/det-vs-cgy/2026/10/24/2026020178).

## Detroit Red Wings at Edmonton Oilers, 9:30 p.m. ET

**Red Wings: unconfirmed.** John Gibson started Saturday's loss at Calgary, making 21 saves on 25 shots, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/det-vs-cgy/2026/10/24/2026020178).

**Oilers: unconfirmed.** Tristan Jarry started Saturday's 7-2 loss to Toronto with 25 saves on 30 shots, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/tor-vs-edm/2026/10/24/2026020176).

**Monday morning update:** still no starters named for any of the six teams. The Rangers signed James Reimer on Sunday ([our story](/articles/rangers-james-reimer-signs-garand-workload-kings-oct-26-2026/)), and the Oilers are without Devon Levi, who is on injured reserve ([our story](/articles/oilers-goalies-without-devon-levi-vs-red-wings-oct-26-2026/)). Devin Cooley has started three straight Flames wins ([our story](/articles/flames-devin-cooley-three-straight-wins-maple-leafs-oct-26-2026/)).
