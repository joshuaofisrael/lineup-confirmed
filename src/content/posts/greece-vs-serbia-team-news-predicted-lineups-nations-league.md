Greece host Serbia tonight, Monday 16 November, in their final Nations League Group A2 match at the Panthessaliko Stadium in Volos. [FotMob](https://www.fotmob.com/matches/greece-vs-serbia/1rcyhf) lists kick-off at 19:45 UTC, which is 7:45pm UK time and 2:45pm ET.

## What is at stake

Greece are second in the group, level on eight points with Germany, after Friday's 7-0 defeat in the Netherlands, [Sports Mole says](https://www.sportsmole.co.uk/football/greece/uefa-nations-league/preview/greece-vs-serbia-prediction-team-news-lineups_609121.html). They need to match Germany's result against the Netherlands to stay in the top two. Serbia have lost all five matches and will finish bottom, but can still avoid automatic relegation to League B through the play-offs depending on results elsewhere.

## Greece team news

Midfielder Dimitrios Kourbelis is expected to miss out with an ankle injury, according to Sports Mole. FotMob also lists Dimitrios Pelkas (knee) as out and Giannis Michailidis (muscle) as doubtful.

**Predicted Greece XI (Sports Mole):** Tzolakis; Rota, Mavropanos, Retsos, Tsimikas; Karetsas, Triantis, Androutsos, Tzolis; Pavlidis, Ioannidis.

FotMob's predicted XI has Charalampos Kostoulas in midfield instead of Nectarios Triantis.

## Serbia team news

Vanja Dragojevic and Andrija Zivkovic are suspended after being sent off in Friday's 3-1 defeat by Germany, both sources say. Sports Mole expects Veljko Paunovic to use a back five, with Stefan Dzodic joining Lazar Samardzic and Sergej Milinkovic-Savic in midfield.

**Predicted Serbia XI (Sports Mole):** V. Milinkovic-Savic; Nedeljkovic, Gudelj, Milenkovic, Babic, Sukacev; Samardzic, Dzodic, S. Milinkovic-Savic; Cvetkovic, Tadic.

FotMob's version pairs Luka Jovic with captain Dusan Tadic up front instead of Mihajlo Cvetkovic. Confirmed team sheets usually arrive about an hour before kick-off.
