The Carolina Hurricanes and Seattle Kraken open the 2026 NHL Global Series Finland at Veikkaus Arena in Helsinki on Thursday at 12 p.m. ET, and [NHL.com's projected lineups](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-lineup-projections-2026-27-season) list Brandon Bussi and Philipp Grubauer first in goal.

Those are projections, not team announcements. At our check at 9:15 a.m. ET, neither club had named a starter.

## Goalie status: Hurricanes vs. Kraken (Thursday, 12 p.m. ET)

- **Hurricanes:** Brandon Bussi, **expected** (listed first in NHL.com's projection, with Pyotr Kochetkov second)
- **Kraken:** Philipp Grubauer, **expected** (listed first in NHL.com's projection, with Joey Daccord second)

TV and streaming listed by NHL.com: HHN, Prime Video, CARNHL, KONG, NHLN, SN+ and TVAS.

## Lineup notes

- **Hurricanes:** Jordan Staal (calf) has practiced twice on the trip but is not expected to play Thursday, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-lineup-projections-2026-27-season). Mike Reilly, Alexander Nikishin, Nicolas Deslauriers and Cayden Primeau are projected scratches.
- **Kraken:** Defenseman Ryan Lindgren has moved from a non-contact jersey to a regular one in practice, but coach Lane Lambert said he is not 100 percent sure Lindgren will be ready on this trip, per the [NHL Status Report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-november-11-2026). Jared McCann (lower body), Jacob Melanson, Curtis Douglas and Lindgren (upper body) are listed injured. Finnish goalie Nikke Kokko is a projected scratch.

## Taylor Hall's 1,000th game

Hall is projected on the Hurricanes' third line with Mark Jankowski and Seth Jarvis. Thursday will be his 1,000th NHL game, making him the first player to reach the mark outside North America, [NHL.com reported](https://www.nhl.com/news/topic/global-series/carolina-hurricanes-seattle-kraken-global-series-finland-perfect-time-for-taylor-hall-1000th-game). He has 793 points in 999 games.

Carolina (14-3-2) enters with the best record in the NHL; Seattle is 9-6-1. The teams meet again in Helsinki on Saturday.

Check the teams' official accounts close to puck drop for the confirmed starters.
