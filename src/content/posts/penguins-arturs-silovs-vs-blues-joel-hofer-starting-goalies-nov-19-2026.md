Both starters are confirmed for Blues at Penguins tonight (7pm ET). "Dan Muse said Arturs Silovs will start in goal tonight," Penguins broadcaster [Dan Potash posted on X](https://x.com/DanPotashTV/status/2123450783836328381), and [Pens Inside Scoop](https://x.com/PensInsideScoop/status/2123451929229500627) posted the same.

## Penguins: Arturs Silovs (confirmed)

[Daily Faceoff](https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/starting-goalies) lists Silovs at 5-6-1 with a 3.35 goals against average and a .873 save percentage. He has matched a career high with two shutouts in 12 games, but Daily Faceoff says he has a 4.30 GAA and .840 save percentage in his last four (0-3-1), and has allowed four or more goals in seven of his 12 games. He allowed four on 24 shots in a 5-2 loss to Anaheim last time out.

Pittsburgh has not played since Saturday's 4-1 loss to Ottawa.

## Blues: Joel Hofer (confirmed)

"Joel Hofer (STL) will start Thursday @ PIT," [Daily Faceoff Fantasy posted](https://x.com/DFOFantasy/status/2123463276604637623), citing Lou Korac. Hofer is 8-6-1 with a 2.27 GAA, a .921 save percentage and two shutouts, according to Daily Faceoff. He allowed four goals on 28 shots in a 4-1 loss to Florida last time, after a six-game stretch with a 1.82 GAA and .938 save percentage.

St. Louis comes in off an 8-6 win over Anaheim.
