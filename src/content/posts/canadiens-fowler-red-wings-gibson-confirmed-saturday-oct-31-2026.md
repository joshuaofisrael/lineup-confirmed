Two more Saturday starters are set, and the late game in Vancouver now has projections.

**Canadiens: Jacob Fowler, confirmed.** "Jacob Fowler vs @penguins," TVA Sports reporter [Renaud Lavoie posted](https://x.com/renlavoietva/status/2116541883962785872) from Montreal's morning skate, and Radio-Canada's [Marc Antoine Godin posted](https://x.com/MAGodin/status/2116541990342947222) the same. Fowler is 6-1-0 with a 1.54 goals-against average, a .942 save percentage and two shutouts in seven starts, and 3-0-0 with two goals allowed on 101 shots during Montreal's four-game winning streak, according to [NHL.com's On Tap](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-on-tap-news-and-notes-october-31-2026). Jakub Dobes made 17 saves in Thursday's 5-2 win at Dallas. The Penguins (5-6-2) have points in three straight. Pittsburgh has not named its goalie.

**Red Wings: John Gibson, confirmed.** "Gibby in net," Red Wings game-day host [Kara Wolfbauer posted](https://x.com/karawolfbauer/status/2116532660927406234) on Saturday morning. NHL.com had projected Gibson, who started Thursday's 4-1 win against Chicago, for the afternoon game against the Blues.

## Maple Leafs at Canucks, 7 p.m. ET

Neither team held a morning skate, so these are projections, not announcements. [NHL.com's projected lineups](https://www.nhl.com/news/toronto-maple-leafs-vancouver-canucks-game-preview-october-31-2026) list:

- **Maple Leafs: Anthony Stolarz, expected,** with Sergei Bobrovsky second. Bobrovsky started Wednesday's 5-4 loss at Seattle.
- **Canucks: Leevi Merilainen, expected,** with Kevin Lankinen second. Thatcher Demko (hip) is injured, and Filip Chytil (shoulder) is out.

The preview also says Chris Tanev returns for Toronto after missing nine games.
