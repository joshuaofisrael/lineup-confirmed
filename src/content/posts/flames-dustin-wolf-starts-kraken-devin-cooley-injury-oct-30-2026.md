**Flames: Dustin Wolf, confirmed.** Wolf starts Friday when Calgary hosts the Seattle Kraken at 8 p.m. ET, Flames radio's [Pat Steinberg posted](https://x.com/SteinbergYYC/status/2116188315888324728) on game day. It is his first start since Oct. 15 at Vegas.

**Kraken: not announced.** Seattle had not named a starter at our last check. Philipp Grubauer started Wednesday's 5-4 home win against Toronto, the [NHL.com box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/tor-vs-sea/2026/10/28/2026020202) shows. Check the [Oct. 30 board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-30/) for updates.

## Why Wolf is back in

Devin Cooley had started every game of Calgary's four-game winning streak. On Wednesday at Edmonton he stopped eight of nine shots before he was hurt in a collision in the crease with 12 seconds left in the first period, [NHL.com's recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/calgary-flames-edmonton-oilers-game-recap-october-28-2026) says. The team called it a lower-body injury. Wolf played the last 40 minutes and made 20 saves on 23 shots in a 4-0 loss.

"You don't want to see anyone get hurt, let alone a guy that's been playing some of his best hockey," Wolf said after that game, per the same recap.

On Friday the Flames [recalled goalie Arsenii Sergeev](https://x.com/NHLFlames/status/2116187201608511728) and forward William Stromgren from the Calgary Wranglers of the American Hockey League. That gives Calgary a second healthy goalie to back up Wolf. The team has not given a timeline for Cooley.

## Where Wolf's season stands

Wolf is 1-4-0 with a 4.81 goals-against average and an .839 save percentage in six appearances, according to his [NHL.com player page](https://www.nhl.com/flames/player/dustin-wolf-8481692/stats). His two starts against Seattle this season, on Oct. 1 and Oct. 4, were both losses. Cooley is 4-3-0 with a .914 save percentage.
