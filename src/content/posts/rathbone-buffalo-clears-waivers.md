*Update, Nov. 20, 2026: [Sportsnet's Nov. 6 story](https://www.sportsnet.ca/nhl/article/rangers-goalie-james-reimer-clears-waivers/) was checked again. It says the Sabres waived Jack Rathbone. It does not say he cleared, and it does not name an assignment.*

Elliotte Friedman reported that Jack Rathbone of the Buffalo Sabres cleared waivers.

In a [Nov. 6, 2026 post](https://x.com/FriedgeHNIC/status/2118781410341232729), the Sportsnet insider wrote:

> "Cleared
>
> Rathbone (BUF) on waivers"

## What Sportsnet reported the same day

Sportsnet's [Nov. 6 story](https://www.sportsnet.ca/nhl/article/rangers-goalie-james-reimer-clears-waivers/) is mostly about James Reimer. In the same piece it says the Sabres waived Rathbone. The details it gives: he was 27, he had not appeared in a game for Buffalo, and his last NHL stint was 11 games with the Vancouver Canucks in 2022-23, with one goal and one assist.

That story does say Reimer cleared and was assigned to the Hartford Wolf Pack. It does not say either of those things for Rathbone. Our [Reimer note](/articles/rangers-james-reimer-clears-waivers/) is the other name on that wire.

## What "cleared" does not settle

A clearance means no other NHL club claimed him on that waiver run. It is not, by itself, a demotion, a recall, or a trade. Friedman's post is the clearance. Sportsnet's story, re-read on Nov. 20, stops at the waiver. Neither source says which roster Rathbone dressed on next, so this page does not either.
