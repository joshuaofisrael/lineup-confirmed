Jeremy Swayman made a season-high 40 saves as the Boston Bruins beat the Florida Panthers 4-1 at TD Garden on Sunday, [NHL.com reported](https://www.nhl.com/news/florida-panthers-boston-bruins-game-recap-november-8-2026). It was Boston's fourth straight win.

The [projected lineups](https://www.nhl.com/news/florida-panthers-boston-bruins-game-preview-november-8-2026) named Swayman and Jacob Markstrom as the starters, and both played.

## Goalie results

| Team | Goalie | Saves | Goals allowed | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bruins | Jeremy Swayman | 40 | 1 | W |
| Panthers | Jacob Markstrom | 32 | 4 | L |

## How the goals came

Boston scored three times in the first period:

- **Elias Lindholm (4:50):** a wrist shot from just inside the blue line. Markstrom dove back and appeared to make the save, but video review overturned the no-goal call.
- **Michael Eyssimont (6:11):** scored off a loose puck after Markstrom stopped his wraparound. Eyssimont had been called up from Providence of the AHL on Saturday.
- **Morgan Geekie (15:59):** a one-timer on a 5-on-3 power play.

Carter Verhaeghe answered on the power play at 18:05. JJ Peterka made it 4-1 58 seconds into the third period after Markstrom stopped a close-range shot from Pavel Zacha and was out of position. David Pastrnak and Zacha each had two assists.

"I think the biggest thing is, they do their job and I do mine," Swayman said. "I just focus on one shot at a time, what comes my way whenever it comes and try to get in front of it."

## Where the teams stand

Boston is 13-4-1 and 8-1-0 at home. Florida is 9-7-2, has lost two straight and is 0-4 in division games. The Panthers were playing the second half of a back-to-back after a 4-3 loss at Washington on Saturday.

## Lineup notes from the preview

- **Bruins:** James Hagens was placed on injured reserve Saturday. Coach Marco Sturm said he will be out for a couple of weeks. Matthew Poitras (upper body) also missed the game.
- **Panthers:** center Anton Lundell was listed as day to day after missing Saturday's loss at Washington.
