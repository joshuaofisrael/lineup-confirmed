Florida Panthers forwards Aleksander Barkov and Brad Marchand skated with the team for the first time since they were injured and could return to the lineup "within two weeks," coach Paul Maurice said Tuesday, according to the [NHL status report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-november-10-2026) published on 11 November.

"'Barky' is six weeks on Thursday; 'Marchy' slightly under six weeks," Maurice said. "(Doctors) said somewhere between six and eight weeks. So, I think we're within two weeks of getting those guys back."

Barkov injured his left knee on 1 October, his second game of the season. The NHL report noted that Florida's captain missed all of last season with a knee injury. Marchand was hurt when he fell into the goal post in the second period of a 2-1 win at the Los Angeles Kings on 6 October.

"It gives everybody a lift to see those guys on the ice," Panthers defenseman Seth Jones said, according to NHL.com.

A return "within two weeks" is the coach's reading of the medical window, not a confirmed activation date. Maurice did not name a specific game for either forward. Lineup decisions will depend on how they progress after that first team skate.

The same NHL status report also covered other clubs' injury news, including returns and activations elsewhere in the league. For Florida, the immediate lineup question is whether Barkov and Marchand can rejoin the lineup before the eight-week end of the range doctors described.
