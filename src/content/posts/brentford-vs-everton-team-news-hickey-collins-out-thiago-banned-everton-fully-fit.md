Brentford go into Monday night's game with Everton short at the back and up front. "Brentford head coach Keith Andrews expects to be without Aaron Hickey and Nathan Collins for 'a number of weeks' after the defenders both suffered injuries over the international break," [Sky Sports News posted on X](https://x.com/SkySportsNews/status/2123801945194299750).

The game is at the Gtech Community Stadium on Monday 23 November, kick-off 8pm UK (3pm ET), live on Sky Sports.

## Brentford team news

**Out:** Aaron Hickey (hamstring), Nathan Collins (knee), Igor Thiago (suspended), Rico Henry (hip and quad), Kaye Furo (metatarsal).

**Could return:** Fábio Carvalho.

Both defenders were hurt on international duty, Hickey for Scotland and Collins for the Republic of Ireland. "No, they won't be available for a number of weeks," Andrews said, according to [Brentford's team news](https://www.brentfordfc.com/en/news/first-team-brentford-v-everton-team-news-aaron-hickey-nathan-collins-injury-update). "Nathan will be out for a couple of weeks or so."

Thiago serves the first game of a three-match ban after Brentford's appeal against his red card at Brighton failed. Everton's preview says veteran Callum Wilson is in line to lead the attack instead. There is better news on Carvalho: "Fábio has every chance of being involved on Monday, which is great news," Andrews said. Josh Dasilva and Antoni Milambo play for Brentford B on Sunday as they build back up.

## Everton team news

**Out:** none reported.

David Moyes expects a clean bill of health. "Hopefully everybody will be fit and available on Monday night for the Brentford game," he said, according to [Everton's team news](https://www.evertonfc.com/news/2026/november/20/moyes-reveals-everton-team-news-for-brentford/). "We've not had anyone return from international duty with any problems."

He hinted at little change in midfield: "the form of Harrison [Armstrong] and Jimmy [Garner] and Kiernan [Dewsbury-Hall] has made it easy to select". Monday will be Moyes's record-breaking 593rd match in charge of Everton, the club says.

## What it means

Without Thiago, Hickey and Collins, Brentford will have a different-looking spine against an Everton side Andrews called "well drilled, well organised". Everton are pushing to stay in the top six, according to the club. Confirmed line-ups are due about an hour before kick-off on Monday.
