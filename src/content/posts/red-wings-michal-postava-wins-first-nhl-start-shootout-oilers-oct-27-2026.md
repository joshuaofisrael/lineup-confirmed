**Result: Postava started and won.** Michal Postava, making his first NHL start, made 25 saves through regulation and overtime and then denied all three Edmonton shooters as the Detroit Red Wings won 4-3 in a shootout at Rogers Place on Monday, [NHL.com reports](https://www.nhl.com/news/detroit-red-wings-edmonton-oilers-game-recap-october-26-2026). Lucas Raymond scored the only shootout goal.

MLive's Ansar Khan [reports](https://www.mlive.com/redwings/2026/10/red-wings-michal-postava-steps-up-in-shootout-to-win-first-nhl-start.html) that the three shooters Postava stopped were Connor McDavid, Leon Draisaitl and Ryan Nugent-Hopkins. He also made a late save on Evan Bouchard with nine seconds left in overtime to get the game to a shootout, according to NHL.com. Dylan Larkin had tied it 3-3 late in the third period with Postava pulled for an extra skater.

## The numbers

- **Postava:** 25 saves (NHL.com and MLive), plus 3-for-3 in the shootout. It was his second NHL game. He debuted in relief of John Gibson in last Thursday's 5-2 win at Vancouver, stopping seven of eight shots ([NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/detroit-red-wings-edmonton-oilers-game-recap-october-26-2026)).
- **Tristan Jarry (Oilers):** 26 saves and the shootout loss, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/detroit-red-wings-edmonton-oilers-game-recap-october-26-2026). Edmonton is 8-2-2.
- **Club history:** NHL.com notes Postava is the third Red Wings goalie in the past 25 years to win his first NHL start, after Petr Mrazek in 2013 and Jimmy Howard in 2005.

## Why Detroit's crease is in flux

Postava, 24, was recalled from AHL Grand Rapids last week after backup Daniil Tarasov was hurt in practice, and he got into his first game when Gibson left the Vancouver game in the third period, [MLive reports](https://www.mlive.com/redwings/2026/10/red-wings-michal-postava-steps-up-in-shootout-to-win-first-nhl-start.html). Gibson dressed as the backup in Edmonton, per the [NHL.com box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/det-vs-edm/2026/10/26/2026020187). Detroit (5-4-3) finished its four-game trip 2-1-1.
