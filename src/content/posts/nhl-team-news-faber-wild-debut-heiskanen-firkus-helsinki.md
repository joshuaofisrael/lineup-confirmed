Saturday's NHL slate has several lineup questions. Here is the team news from Friday's [NHL.com status report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-november-13-2026) and other reports.

## Wild: Faber could return in Boston

Defenseman Brock Faber could make his season debut against the Boston Bruins on Saturday, Wild coach John Hynes said, according to NHL.com. Faber has not played since sustaining a skull fracture in a preseason game on 25 September and had surgery two days later. Hynes said it is not guaranteed, but if Faber does not play Saturday he expects him back soon after, [Hockey.com reports](https://www.hockey.com/news/wild-coach-outlines-brock-faber-s-return-timeline-b1b34m). [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/hockey/news/wilds-brock-faber-targeting-saturday-return/), citing The Athletic, says Faber has told the Wild he is ready to play.

## Stars: Heiskanen out again

Miro Heiskanen will not return against the Buffalo Sabres on Saturday (8 p.m. ET) but could play on Sunday, NHL.com reports. He has missed three straight games with an upper-body injury. Stars coach Glen Gulutzan said defenseman Lian Bichsel will be out "a couple of weeks" with a lower-body injury.

## Kraken: Firkus could debut in Helsinki

Seattle may give forward Jagger Firkus his NHL debut against the Carolina Hurricanes at the NHL Global Series in Helsinki at 1 p.m. ET. The 22-year-old has 12 points in 12 AHL games for Coachella Valley this season.

## Other notes

- **Canadiens:** Chris Kreider appeared to injure his left leg in Thursday's 5-2 win over Boston and did not return. There was no update after the game. Montreal hosts Colorado on Saturday.
- **Jets:** Dylan DeMelo was placed on injured reserve and is week to week. Henry Thrun was recalled and will play against Chicago, and Isak Rosen is out.
- **Devils:** Johnathan Kovacevic was placed on injured reserve with an ankle injury, and defenseman Anton Silayev was recalled from Utica.
