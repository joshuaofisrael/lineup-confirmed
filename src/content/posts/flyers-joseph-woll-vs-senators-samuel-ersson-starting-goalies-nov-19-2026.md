Both starters are confirmed for Flyers at Senators tonight (7pm ET). "Lineup update for #PHIvsOTT: Joseph Woll gets the start between the pipes," the [Philadelphia Flyers posted on X](https://x.com/NHLFlyers/status/2123454866135629928).

## Flyers: Joseph Woll (confirmed)

Flyers reporter [Charlie O'Connor posted](https://x.com/charlieo_conn/status/2123457910558916688) that there are no lineup changes, and that head coach Rick Tocchet "wanted to give Woll another start because he's been playing well (cited his 3 straight wins) and also that Vladar played well vs. MTL last year. So Vladar goes Saturday."

[Daily Faceoff](https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/starting-goalies) lists Woll at 4-6-2 with a 2.92 goals against average and a .899 save percentage. It says he has won three straight with a 2.30 GAA and .900 save percentage, and saved 16 of 19 shots in his last outing, a 4-3 win over Buffalo.

## Senators: Samuel Ersson (confirmed)

"Sam Ersson will start tonight vs PHI," [Coming In Hot posted](https://x.com/ComingInHotSens/status/2123451868181361080), and Daily Faceoff has him confirmed against his former team. Ersson is 3-3-2 with a 3.11 GAA and .885 save percentage. Daily Faceoff says he has not played since Linus Ullmark returned from a lower-body injury earlier this month, but posted a 1.98 GAA and .925 save percentage over a five-game stretch in which Ottawa took at least a point each night (3-0-2).

Ottawa is 5-0-1 in its past six games, according to Daily Faceoff.
