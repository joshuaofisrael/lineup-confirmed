Dallas Stars defenseman Miro Heiskanen will not play against the Winnipeg Jets on Thursday (8 p.m. ET), [NHL.com's Status Report said](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-november-11-2026), and the Utah Mammoth have moved two regulars to injured reserve.

## Stars: Heiskanen

Heiskanen will miss his third straight game. He left early in the second period of a 6-5 win at Washington on Nov. 3 after he was hit into the boards. He skated on his own Wednesday and could return this weekend, according to NHL.com.

"We hope maybe on the weekend but it's definitely not tomorrow," coach Glen Gulutzan said, per the [Status Report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-november-11-2026). Heiskanen has 11 points in 15 games and averages 24:53 of ice time.

## Mammoth: Guenther, Schmidt, Cooley

Before Wednesday's 5-3 loss at Vegas, Utah placed forward Dylan Guenther on injured reserve with an upper-body injury, and he is expected to miss two to four weeks, [NHL.com reported](https://www.nhl.com/news/utah-mammoth-vegas-golden-knights-game-recap-november-11-2026). Defenseman Nate Schmidt went on injured reserve retroactive to Nov. 5.

- **Logan Cooley:** missed Wednesday's game, day to day (upper body).
- **Kailer Yamamoto:** activated off injured reserve and played 18:13.
- **Daniil But:** recalled from Tucson of the AHL and played 12:36.
- **Nick Schmaltz:** left Wednesday's game in the second period with an undisclosed injury and did not return, per the [recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/utah-mammoth-vegas-golden-knights-game-recap-november-11-2026).

Sebastian Cossa started in goal for Utah and made 23 saves.

## Also on the injury list

- **Senators:** Nick Cousins is out 7-10 days after he was helped off the ice late in Tuesday's game against Washington, per NHL.com.
- **Golden Knights:** Defenseman Noah Hanifin is out week to week with an undisclosed injury, coach Ryan Craig said.
- **Lightning:** Brayden Point remains on injured reserve and will not play at Columbus on Thursday. See our earlier story on [Point and Sam O'Reilly](/articles/lightning-place-point-on-ir-oreilly-debut/).
