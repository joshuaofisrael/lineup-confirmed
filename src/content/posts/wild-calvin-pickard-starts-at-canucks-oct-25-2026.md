**Wild: Calvin Pickard — confirmed.** Minnesota coach John Hynes announced the goalie plan for the weekend back-to-back: Jesper Wallstedt started Saturday at the Seattle Kraken, and Pickard starts Sunday against the Vancouver Canucks (10 p.m. ET), [The Hockey News reports](https://thehockeynews.com/nhl/minnesota-wild/game-day/wild-announce-goalie-plan-for-saturday-sundays-back-to-back). [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/hockey/news/wilds-calvin-pickard-tending-twine-sunday/) attributes the same plan to Joe Smith of The Athletic.

It is only the second start of the season for the 34-year-old Pickard. In his lone outing he allowed five goals in a loss to the Winnipeg Jets on Oct. 13, per [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/hockey/news/wilds-calvin-pickard-tending-twine-sunday/), and [The Hockey News](https://thehockeynews.com/nhl/minnesota-wild/game-day/wild-announce-goalie-plan-for-saturday-sundays-back-to-back) lists him at 0-1-0 with a 5.00 goals-against average and an .800 save percentage. Wallstedt had started 10 of Minnesota's first 11 games before Saturday, going 6-2-2 with a 2.19 GAA and a .915 save percentage, according to the same report.

Pickard has history on his side in this matchup: he is 2-2-2 with a 2.48 GAA and a .909 save percentage in six career games against the Canucks, [The Hockey News notes](https://thehockeynews.com/nhl/minnesota-wild/game-day/wild-announce-goalie-plan-for-saturday-sundays-back-to-back).

**Canucks: unconfirmed.** Vancouver had not named its starter in sources we checked. We will update the [NHL starting goalies board](/nhl/starting-goalies/) when it does. Nothing here is betting advice.
