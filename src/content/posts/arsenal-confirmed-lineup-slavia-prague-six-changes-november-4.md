## What changed from Anfield

Arteta's team news explains the six changes from the side that started at Liverpool. Martin Zubimendi and Mikel Merino started in midfield, with Merino returning to a venue where he scored twice in a 3–0 win a year earlier, Arsenal note. Piero Hincapié received his first Champions League start of the season at left-back, while Ben White replaced Jurriën Timber on the right of defence.

Eberechi Eze started with a chance to score in a third European game in a row, and Viktor Gyökeres was preferred to Kai Havertz up front after Havertz's winner against Liverpool three days earlier. Havertz, Martin Ødegaard, Timber, Riccardo Calafiori, Bruno Guimarães and Christos Tzolis dropped to the bench from the Anfield XI.

The rotation therefore touches defence, midfield and attack rather than changing only one department. Keeping Rice and Gabriel in the side preserved a familiar defensive spine while resting other regulars after Premier League duty.

## Saliba's return to the matchday squad

William Saliba was named among the substitutes and, Arsenal say, was looking for his first minutes of the campaign after recovering from a back injury. Inclusion on the bench confirms he was judged available for selection; it is not by itself proof of a full ninety minutes or of a completed recovery timeline beyond what the club stated. Having both Arrizabalaga and Meslier among the goalkeeping options on the bench likewise reflects matchday depth listed by the club, not a prediction about who would replace Raya if needed.
