**Devils: unconfirmed for Tuesday at Dallas.** Connor Hellebuyck practiced with the New Jersey Devils for the first time Monday and told reporters he could play right away, [NHL.com's Mike G. Morreale reports](https://www.nhl.com/news/connor-hellebuyck-says-he-is-ready-today-to-make-new-jersey-devils-debut). "If you're asking me, I'm ready today," he said, before adding that he does not make every decision.

Coach Sheldon Keefe did not name a starter for Tuesday's game against the Stars (8 p.m. ET). He said the intent is to get Hellebuyck involved on this road trip but that the team will "take it a day at a time," according to NHL.com. Hellebuyck still has to clear the club's medical and coaching checkpoints after missing all of training camp and the start of the season.

## Why it matters this week

New Jersey (7-4-0) plays three road games in four nights: at Dallas on Tuesday, at Colorado on Wednesday and at Vegas on Friday. Tuesday and Wednesday are a back-to-back, so the Devils are likely to need two goalies in two nights whatever they decide about Hellebuyck.

Jake Allen started Saturday's game against the Kings, per the [NHL.com box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/lak-vs-njd/2026/10/24/2026020169). Hellebuyck arrived in Friday's trade with Winnipeg. The Jets received forwards Dawson Mercer and Nick Bjugstad, goalie Nico Daws, a prospect's rights and two draft picks, [NHL.com reported](https://www.nhl.com/news/winnipeg-jets-will-reset-after-connor-hellebuyck-traded-to-new-jersey-devils). He had been suspended by Winnipeg after not reporting to camp. NHL.com notes he has won the Vezina Trophy three times and the Hart Trophy for 2024-25.
