Spain host England today, Sunday 15 November, in the final round of Nations League group games, with top spot in Group A3 at stake. Kick-off at the Metropolitano in Madrid is 7:45pm UK time (2:45pm ET), [according to The Standard](https://www.standard.co.uk/sport/football/england-xi-vs-spain-predicted-lineup-confirmed-team-news-injury-latest-nations-league-2026-today-b1304855.html). [England Football](https://www.englandfootball.com/articles/2026/Nov/14/spain-england-uefa-nations-league-thomas-tuchel-press-conference-20261411) says a win would lift England above Spain into first place; both sides have already qualified for the March quarter-finals.

## England team news

Thomas Tuchel confirmed at his press conference that James Trafford will start in goal, with the goalkeepers sharing matches as in the previous campaign. "James Trafford will play tomorrow," he said. Ezri Konsa and Djed Spence are fit again after missing Thursday's 2-1 win over Croatia, giving Tuchel "full choice" in his back four. [Sports Mole](https://www.sportsmole.co.uk/football/england/uefa-nations-league/news/one-change-two-boosts-tuchel-delivers-england-team-news-update-for-spain_609119.html) adds that Myles Lewis-Skelly is back after a yellow-card suspension, and that Jordan Pickford, who played against Croatia, drops out.

**Predicted England XI (The Standard):** Trafford; Quansah, Konsa, Burn, Hall; Rice, Anderson; Rogers, Bellingham, Gordon; Kane.

## Spain team news

Lamine Yamal and Mikel Oyarzabal are back in the squad after missing Thursday's 2-0 win over Czechia, [EFE reports](https://efe.com/deportes/2026-11-15/descartes-espana-inglaterra-liga-naciones/), with Yamal getting the number 10 shirt back. Alejandro Grimaldo left the camp with flu, and Luis de la Fuente left out Jorge de Frutos, Roberto Fernández, Jacobo Ramón and goalkeeper Álvaro Valles. Aymeric Laporte returns to the squad.

[Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/spain-vs-england-predicted-lineup-054900555.html) says Rodri (hamstring), Dani Olmo and Eric García are out, and that Spain are unbeaten in 43 matches.

**Predicted Spain XI (Yahoo Sports):** Simón; Porro, Cubarsí, Huijsen, Cucurella; Ruiz, Pedri; Yamal, López, Baena; Oyarzabal.
