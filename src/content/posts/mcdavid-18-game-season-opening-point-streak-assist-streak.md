Connor McDavid has recorded a point in each of the Edmonton Oilers' first 18 games of the 2026-27 season, NHL Public Relations said on Saturday night, 7 November, after Edmonton played the Vancouver Canucks.

In a [post on X](https://x.com/NHLPR/status/2119276270467108952), the league's PR account said McDavid is the sixth player in NHL history to record "a season-opening run of at least that length."

## Chasing Gretzky's assist mark

All 18 games have included an assist. NHL Public Relations [said](https://x.com/NHLPR/status/2119272136711999702) the streak ties Adam Oates, who had an 18-game run in 1992-93, for the second-longest assist streak in NHL history. Only Wayne Gretzky, with 23 straight games in 1990-91, has gone longer. McDavid needs five more games with an assist to match Gretzky.
