**Kraken: Philipp Grubauer, expected.** Grubauer "should be back in there Friday for a second straight game based of the morning skate," the Kraken's Geoff Baker wrote in the [team's game preview](https://www.nhl.com/kraken/news/seattle-kraken-at-calgary-flames-game-preview-projected-lines-103026). NHL.com's [projected lineups](https://www.nhl.com/news/seattle-kraken-calgary-flames-game-preview-october-30-2026) also list Grubauer first in goal, with Joey Daccord second. Seattle has not formally announced a starter, so he stays expected on the [Oct. 30 board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-30/) until the team or a reporter at the rink confirms it.

**Flames: Dustin Wolf, confirmed.** Wolf starts at home, Flames radio's [Pat Steinberg posted](https://x.com/SteinbergYYC/status/2116188315888324728). Puck drop is 8 p.m. ET at Scotiabank Saddledome.

## Grubauer's recent run

Grubauer started Wednesday's 5-4 home win against Toronto and made 18 saves on 22 shots, the [NHL.com box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/tor-vs-sea/2026/10/28/2026020202) shows. His [NHL.com player page](https://www.nhl.com/player/philipp-grubauer-8475831) lists him at 5-1-1 with a 2.93 goals-against average and a .898 save percentage in eight games. The Kraken preview notes he is 5-0-1 in his last six outings.

Seattle (8-4-1) has won three of its past four games, NHL.com says. The Kraken beat Calgary 6-1 twice early this season.

## Calgary's side

Devin Cooley is week to week with a lower-body injury and was placed on injured reserve Friday, per the [NHL.com preview](https://www.nhl.com/news/seattle-kraken-calgary-flames-game-preview-october-30-2026). Arsenii Sergeev, recalled from the AHL on Friday, is projected as Wolf's backup. Our [Wolf story](/articles/flames-dustin-wolf-starts-kraken-devin-cooley-injury-oct-30-2026/) has more on the Cooley injury.

Calgary plays again Saturday at Colorado. No Flames starter is named for that game yet; it is unconfirmed on the [Oct. 31 board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-31/).
