The Edmonton Oilers host the Detroit Red Wings at 9:30 p.m. ET Monday with a thinner goaltending group than they started the week with.

**Oilers: unconfirmed.** On Oct. 23 the Oilers [placed Devon Levi on injured reserve](https://www.nhl.com/oilers/news/release-oilers-place-levi-on-ir-tomkins-recalled-on-emergency-basis) and recalled Matt Tomkins from AHL Bakersfield under emergency conditions. The team says Levi was hurt in Thursday's win over Carolina and will miss time. Before the injury he was 6-1-0 with a 3.11 goals-against average and an .887 save percentage in seven starts.

That leaves Tristan Jarry as Edmonton's most experienced healthy goalie. He started Saturday's 7-2 home loss to Toronto, per the [NHL.com box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/tor-vs-edm/2026/10/24/2026020176). Tomkins, 32, was 1-2-0 with a .909 save percentage in three AHL starts this season. According to the club, all six of his NHL appearances came with Tampa Bay in 2023-24.

The [Oilers' game preview](https://www.nhl.com/oilers/news/preview-oilers-vs-red-wings-10-26-26) does not name Monday's starter. It quotes coach Mike Babcock saying of Saturday's loss, "Tonight wasn't good enough, period."

**Red Wings: unconfirmed.** John Gibson started Saturday's 4-1 loss at Calgary. Edmonton's preview puts him at 3-4-0 with an .867 save percentage and a 3.44 goals-against average. Monday is the last game of Detroit's four-game western road trip. The Red Wings have not announced a starter.

Starters will be marked confirmed on the [Oct. 26 board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-26/) once the teams announce them. Nothing here is betting advice.
