**Canadiens: Jakub Dobes, confirmed.** Coach Martin St. Louis confirmed after Wednesday's practice that Dobes will start Thursday against the Dallas Stars, the [Canadiens' practice note](https://www.nhl.com/canadiens/news/lines-at-practice-oct-28-2026) says. Both Dobes and Jacob Fowler took part in the session at StarCenter in Farmers Branch. Puck drop is 8 p.m. ET at American Airlines Center. It is the last game of Montreal's four-game road trip. The [NHL schedule feed](https://api-web.nhle.com/v1/schedule/2026-10-29) lists the same game, Montreal at Dallas, at 8 p.m. ET.

The club's note names the starter. It does not give a reason. We are not adding one.

## Tuesday was Fowler's

Jacob Fowler started Tuesday at St. Louis and made an NHL-career-high 46 saves in a 2-0 win, [NHL.com's recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/montreal-canadiens-st-louis-blues-game-recap-october-27-2026) says. It was his second shutout of the season and his sixth straight win. He has allowed one goal or fewer in each of his past three starts. Zachary Bolduc and Florian Xhekaj scored. Xhekaj's was his first NHL goal. Jordan Binnington made 18 saves for the Blues.

Fowler also practiced Wednesday. Thursday's assignment is Dobes.

## Stars: unconfirmed

**Dallas has no goalie named for Thursday in the notes above.** Casey DeSmith started Tuesday against New Jersey and made 22 saves in a 5-1 loss, the [NHL.com recap of that game](https://www.nhl.com/news/new-jersey-devils-dallas-stars-game-recap-october-27-2026) says. That was Tuesday. It is not a Thursday assignment.

The [Oct. 29 status report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-october-28-2026) discusses Dallas injuries and says forward Sam Steel is likely to return Thursday. It does not name a goalie. The Stars' side of the [Oct. 29 board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-29/) stays unconfirmed until the team or a reporter names one.
