## The two back-to-backs

**Oilers.** Edmonton plays at the Islanders at 2 p.m. ET on Saturday, then in Manhattan on Sunday. [NHL.com's projected lineups](https://www.nhl.com/news/edmonton-oilers-new-york-islanders-game-preview-october-31-2026) list Tristan Jarry for Saturday, so Sunday's starter will depend on how that goes and what the Oilers announce.

**Senators.** Ottawa plays at San Jose at 4 p.m. ET on Saturday, then at Anaheim on Sunday. [NHL.com projects](https://www.nhl.com/news/ottawa-senators-san-jose-sharks-game-preview-october-31-2026) Jonas Johansson for Saturday.

## Rangers: Korpisalo returns to practice

Joonas Korpisalo practiced with the Rangers on Friday for the first time in the regular season, a "big step" in his recovery from a lower-body injury, coach Mike Sullivan said, per [NHL.com's status report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-october-31-2026). He did not finish practice, which was by design under a plan with the team's medical staff. Dylan Garand and James Reimer were the other goalies on the ice. The Rangers have not played since Monday, when Reimer allowed three goals on eight shots before Garand relieved him, so both are rested.

## Jets in Raleigh

Winnipeg has lost eight straight (0-6-2), and Daws made his first Jets start on Friday. See our [Friday goalie recap](/articles/devils-hellebuyck-second-win-jets-daws-first-start-oct-31-2026/) for both sides of the Hellebuyck trade.
