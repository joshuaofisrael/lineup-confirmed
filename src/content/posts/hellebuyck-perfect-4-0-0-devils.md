The NHL said Connor Hellebuyck is a perfect four wins, zero losses, and zero overtime losses since joining the New Jersey Devils.

In a [6 November 2026 post](https://x.com/NHL/status/2118550164806012998), the league put the streak in all caps with a graphic:

> "CONNOR HELLEBUYCK IS A PERFECT 4-0-0 SINCE JOINING THE DEVILS"

That record covers his starts after the late October trade that sent him from Winnipeg to New Jersey.

## What the streak means for the crease

Four straight wins does not lock every future start by itself, but it does show how quickly New Jersey has leaned on the veteran. Lineup Confirmed has already tracked his early Devils appearances, including the debut win at Dallas and the follow up in Vegas. The league's perfect record note is the clean public tally as of that post.

For fantasy and starter projection readers, treat the NHL graphic as confirmation of the win count, then still wait for the team's game day confirmation before assuming Hellebuyck is in against the next opponent. Hot goalies get the net until they do not; the league has simply marked that he has not lost in a Devils sweater yet.
