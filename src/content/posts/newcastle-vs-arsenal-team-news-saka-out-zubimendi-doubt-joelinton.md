Newcastle United host Arsenal at St James' Park on Saturday 21 November, the first Premier League match for both clubs after the international break. Kick-off is 5:30pm UK time (12:30pm ET), live on Sky Sports, according to the [Premier League's November fixture list](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4725574/fixture-amendments-for-premier-league-matches-in-november).

This is early team news, compiled before Thursday and Friday's press conferences. We will publish the confirmed lineups when the teams are announced.

## Arsenal team news

- **Bukayo Saka (quad):** expected to stay out until December, according to [Fantasy Football Scout's early Gameweek 11 round-up](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/11/19/fpl-gameweek-11-early-team-news-injury-latest-on-all-20-teams/).
- **Max Dowman:** serves the third game of a three-match ban.
- **Martin Zubimendi (hamstring):** the new worry. He came off in Spain's Nations League defeat by England on Sunday, seemingly clutching his hamstring. There was no update on the extent of the problem when the round-up was published. Our [Spain vs England team news](/articles/spain-vs-england-team-news-predicted-lineups-nations-league/) covers how that game was set up.

For a reminder of how Arsenal lined up the last time they rotated, see our [Arsenal lineup against Slavia Prague](/articles/arsenal-confirmed-lineup-slavia-prague-six-changes-november-4/).

## Newcastle team news

- **Joelinton (hamstring):** looks set to miss out again. He was still absent when Newcastle trained on Monday.
- **Ewen Jaouen (hamstring):** also expected to miss out.
- **Nico Gonzalez and Joe Willock:** missed that session through illness only.
- **Jacob Ramsey:** available again after serving a one-match suspension.

## What to watch for

The biggest call is in Arsenal's midfield. If Zubimendi is not fit, Arsenal would be without him as well as Saka. For Newcastle, the return of Ramsey gives them an extra option while Joelinton is out.
