A back-to-back is two games on consecutive days. The goalie who starts the first game is a fact about that game. The goalie for the second game is a separate fact, and it stays unconfirmed here until a team, the league, or an on-site reporter says who it is. This is how this desk labels starters. It is not a league rule, and it is not a prediction.

This week's slate is a clean set of examples. Two clubs changed goalies on the second night, and both changes are in the game stories. A third club has played the first night and has not named anyone for the second.

## When the second starter is in the recap

**Flyers.** Tuesday's starter was Dan Vladar, as our [report from that morning](/articles/flyers-dan-vladar-starts-blue-jackets-capitals-back-to-back-oct-27-2026/) said. On Wednesday, the second night, Joseph Woll started at Washington and made 25 saves in a 5-2 loss, [NHL.com's recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/philadelphia-flyers-washington-capitals-game-recap-october-28-2026) says. The Flyers had lost 4-3 in overtime to Columbus the night before. More on Thompson's return is in the [Capitals story](/articles/capitals-logan-thompson-returns-starts-flyers-oct-28-2026/).

**Devils.** Connor Hellebuyck started Tuesday at Dallas, his first game for New Jersey, and made 17 saves in a 5-1 win, [NHL.com reports](https://www.nhl.com/news/new-jersey-devils-dallas-stars-game-recap-october-27-2026). Casey DeSmith made 22 saves for the Stars. On Wednesday in Colorado, Jake Allen started and made 40 saves on 45 shots in a 5-2 loss, per the [NHL.com box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/njd-vs-col/2026/10/28/2026020201). (The NHL.com recap gives 39; we use the box score.) Mackenzie Blackwood made 20 saves in his 300th NHL game, the [Avalanche recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/new-jersey-devils-colorado-avalanche-game-recap-october-28-2026) says.

"That's a tough back-to-back," Devils coach Sheldon Keefe said after the loss.

Hellebuyck on Tuesday and Allen on Wednesday are both confirmed, because the recaps say they started. The Tuesday plan is in our [Hellebuyck debut story](/articles/devils-connor-hellebuyck-starts-debut-at-stars-oct-27-2026/).

## When the second night is still open

**Capitals.** Thompson started Wednesday and made 23 saves in the 5-2 win, the [status report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-october-28-2026) says. Washington plays at Nashville on Thursday at 8 p.m. ET, the [NHL schedule feed](https://api-web.nhle.com/v1/schedule/2026-10-29) shows. That is the second night. The same status report does not name a Nashville starter. Wednesday's result does not fill Thursday in. The [Oct. 29 board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-29/) keeps Washington unconfirmed until someone does.

The label on each of those nights is the whole point. Confirmed means a team, the league, or a reporter at the rink said he starts, or the box score shows he did. Expected is a projection, such as a morning-skate crease note, that is not an announcement. Unconfirmed means we do not have that word yet. A back-to-back explains why the question comes up. It does not answer it. Nothing here is betting advice.
