Nick Suzuki returned to the Montreal Canadiens lineup against the Utah Mammoth on Thursday, while Taylor Hall also made his return for the Carolina Hurricanes, according to the NHL’s latest status report.

Suzuki had missed six games with an upper body injury. Before the injury, the Montreal captain had played 546 consecutive games to begin his NHL career. He entered the season with 12 points, including five goals and seven assists, in nine appearances, following a career high 101 point campaign last season.

Hall returned against the New York Islanders after missing nine games with two separate upper body injuries. He had recorded five points, three goals and two assists, in eight games before his absence.

The report also covered several other teams. Dallas defenceman Miro Heiskanen missed Thursday’s game against Columbus after leaving a contest earlier in the week. Coach Glen Gulutzan described him as day to day, with the possibility of a return for the Stars’ next game against San Jose on Saturday.

Minnesota defenceman Brock Faber practised for the first time since sustaining a skull fracture in a pre season game on 25 September. Coach John Hynes said the timing of his return remained uncertain as the player works towards being physically ready.

The NHL report also noted returns for St Louis defenceman Colton Parayko and Pittsburgh forward Evgeni Malkin. Toronto forward John Tavares had been upgraded to day to day, while New York Islanders forward Mathew Barzal continued practising in a non contact jersey without a return timetable.

The updates offer a snapshot of several clubs managing key players as the season progresses. Suzuki’s return is particularly notable for Montreal after the end of his long consecutive games streak, while Hall’s availability gives Carolina another experienced forward back in the lineup.
