Minnesota Wild general manager Bill Guerin said the team could keep Quinn Hughes past the NHL Trade Deadline on March 1 even if the defenseman has not signed a new contract, [NHL.com reported](https://www.nhl.com/news/quinn-hughes-could-stay-with-minnesota-wild-past-trade-deadline-if-unsigned).

Guerin said nothing has changed in talks with Hughes and said he wants Hughes to focus on hockey. Asked if he would keep him past the deadline, he said yes.

"We're a better team with Quinn Hughes," Guerin said, per NHL.com.

## The contract picture

Hughes, 27, is in the final season of a six-year, $47.1 million contract ($7.85 million average annual value) he signed with Vancouver in 2021, according to [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/quinn-hughes-could-stay-with-minnesota-wild-past-trade-deadline-if-unsigned). He has been eligible to sign an extension since July 1 and can become an unrestricted free agent on July 1, 2027, if he does not sign by June 30.

Minnesota acquired him from the Canucks on Dec. 12, 2025. [TSN reported](https://www.tsn.ca/nhl/article/wild-gm-guerin-says-hes-open-to-keeping-an-unsigned-hughes-beyond-trade-deadline/) the Wild sent forwards Liam Ohgren and Marco Rossi, defenseman Zeev Buium and a 2026 first-round pick in that trade. On Oct. 24, Hughes said something could happen soon, per TSN, but Guerin's update suggests no deal is close.

## On the ice

Hughes has 26 points (six goals) in 19 games and averages 28:07 of ice time, second highest in the league, according to TSN. Since the trade he has 80 points in 68 games, per NHL.com.

The Wild (13-3-4) lead the Central Division and pushed their point streak to nine games (7-0-2) with a 2-1 overtime win at Montreal on Tuesday.

**What it means for the lineup:** Nothing changes in the short term. Hughes remains Minnesota's top defenseman, and Guerin's comments make a deadline sale look less likely, though he did not set a date for a decision.
