Fenerbahçe host Liverpool in the UEFA Champions League on Wednesday, November 4, with Nathan Aké and Marco Asensio ruled out for the Turkish side and both teams' starting lineups still unconfirmed. UEFA has published predicted XIs for the league-phase match, scheduled for 17:45 UK time (18:45 CET), but those selections should not be mistaken for official team sheets.

The fixture brings together a Liverpool side that have won all three of their Champions League matches this season and a Fenerbahçe team looking to reverse a difficult recent run. According to [LiveScore's matchday-four preview](https://www.livescore.com/en/news/football/champions-league/predictions/champions-league-predictions-all-nine-matchday-four-games-on-wednesday/), the hosts have lost consecutive matches and won just once in their last five outings.

## Fenerbahçe injury news: Aké and Asensio unavailable

Fenerbahçe will be without defender Nathan Aké because of a hamstring injury and attacking midfielder Marco Asensio because of a knee problem. Both absences appear in [UEFA's matchday-four team-news guide](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02ab-21f9907f0a27-6a6a67e9775d-1000--champions-league-starting-and-predicted-line-ups-matchday-4-/) and LiveScore's preview.

There is also uncertainty over Kojo Peprah Oppong. LiveScore reports that he was forced off during Fenerbahçe's weekend defeat to Göztepe, while UEFA lists him as doubtful with an unspecified issue. His availability therefore remains unresolved in the published pre-match information.

The hosts are also preparing under interim manager Dirk Kuyt following İsmail Kartal's departure. Kuyt's Liverpool connection adds another storyline: LiveScore notes that the former Reds player made nearly 300 appearances for the English club and scored in the 2007 Champions League final.

## Liverpool injury news: Four players remain sidelined

Liverpool have no fresh injury concerns in LiveScore's preview, although four existing absentees remain unavailable. Conor Bradley is out with a knee injury, Hugo Ekitiké has an Achilles problem, Jeremie Frimpong is sidelined by a hamstring injury and Giovanni Leoni is missing with a knee injury.

UEFA lists no additional doubtful Liverpool players in its pre-match team-news section. That does not establish a final starting XI: the published selection is a prediction, and the club's official lineup has yet to be confirmed.

## Predicted Fenerbahçe vs Liverpool lineups

[UEFA's predicted lineups](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02ab-21f9907f0a27-6a6a67e9775d-1000--champions-league-starting-and-predicted-line-ups-matchday-4-/) offer the following possible starting teams, subject to change before kickoff.

**Liverpool predicted XI:** Alisson Becker; Szoboszlai, Jacquet, Van Dijk, Tsimikas; Gravenberch, Mac Allister; Muñoz, Wirtz, Barcola; Isak.

These are projections, not confirmed selections. In particular, the names appearing in UEFA's guide should not be presented as players officially chosen to start until the matchday teams are announced.

## Form and European record ahead of kickoff

Liverpool arrive with a perfect three-win record in this season's Champions League, but their historical record away to Turkish opposition offers a contrasting perspective. LiveScore reports that Liverpool have never won away against a Turkish club in the European Cup or Champions League.

Across 11 previous meetings with Turkish teams in Europe's leading club competition, Liverpool have recorded four victories, all at home, according to the same preview. Their famous 2005 Champions League triumph in Istanbul is a separate part of the club's history, rather than evidence of an away win against Turkish opposition.

LiveScore forecasts a 4-1 Liverpool victory, but that is the outlet's prediction rather than a result or an established expectation. With Fenerbahçe missing Aké and Asensio, Oppong's fitness unresolved and both official lineups still pending, the final team announcements remain the key outstanding information before Wednesday's match.
