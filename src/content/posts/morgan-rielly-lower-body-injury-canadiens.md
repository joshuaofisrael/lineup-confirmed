Toronto Maple Leafs defenceman Morgan Rielly left Saturday's game against the Montreal Canadiens with a lower-body injury and did not return.

NHL insider Chris Johnston [posted during the game](https://x.com/reporterchris/status/2119239106668466494) on 7 November 2026:

> "Morgan Rielly is done for the night due to a lower-body injury."

## What we know

[TSN reported](https://www.tsn.ca/nhl/article/maple-leafs-d-rielly-exits-game-with-lower-body-injury/) that Rielly played 5:45 before leaving, and that head coach Jim Hiller did not have an update on his status after the 4-2 loss. TSN said the 32-year-old has 12 assists in 18 games this season.

## Lineup impact

The loss left Toronto 8-8-2, according to TSN, two games into a six-game road trip. The Leafs play the New Jersey Devils on Sunday, then the Colorado Avalanche on Tuesday, the Minnesota Wild on Thursday and the Edmonton Oilers on Saturday.

No diagnosis or timeline has been given. Lineup Confirmed readers should wait for Toronto's morning update or warm-up lines before assuming Rielly sits against New Jersey. The confirmed facts for now are Johnston's [in-game report](https://x.com/reporterchris/status/2119239106668466494) and TSN's post-game write-up.
