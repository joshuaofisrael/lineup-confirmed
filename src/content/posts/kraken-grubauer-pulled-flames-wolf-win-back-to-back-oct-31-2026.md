**Dustin Wolf, Flames: 20 saves on 22 shots, win.** Calgary beat Seattle 6-2 at Scotiabank Saddledome on Friday, the [NHL.com box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/sea-vs-cgy/2026/10/30/2026020216) shows. It was the Flames' fifth straight home win and fifth in their past six games, per [NHL.com's recap](https://www.nhl.com/news/seattle-kraken-calgary-flames-game-recap-october-30-2026). Matt Coronato had a goal and three assists.

**Philipp Grubauer, Kraken: 10 of 14, pulled.** Grubauer started for a second straight game and was replaced by Joey Daccord at 10:40 of the second period. Daccord made 11 saves in relief. "I would say probably the worst 40 minutes I've been a part of since I got here," Kraken coach Lane Lambert said of the last two periods, per the recap.

## Calgary's back-to-back

The Flames play at Colorado at 10 p.m. ET on Saturday, the [NHL schedule](https://www.nhl.com/schedule/2026-10-31) shows, and had not named a starter at our last check. Devin Cooley is week to week with a lower-body injury from a crease collision on Wednesday at Edmonton, [NHL.com's status report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-october-31-2026) says. The Flames could go back to Wolf on consecutive nights or turn to another goalie; we will post whoever the team or a reporter at the rink names.

Colorado has not named its starter either. Mackenzie Blackwood started the Avalanche's last game, a 5-2 win against New Jersey on Wednesday, making 20 saves on 22 shots per the [NHL.com box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/njd-vs-col/2026/10/28/2026020201).

Both teams are on the [Oct. 31 board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-31/), which we update as starters are named. Seattle does not play again until next week. Nothing here is betting advice.
