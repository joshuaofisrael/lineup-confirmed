## Flyers at Capitals, 7:30 p.m. ET

**Capitals: unconfirmed.** Logan Thompson (lower body) took part in the first 40 minutes of Monday's practice, [NHL.com's status report says](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-october-26-2026). On Tuesday he was warming up for a second straight practice, NHL.com's Tom Gulitti [reported](https://x.com/TomGulittiNHL/status/2115087414632788088), and the [Oct. 27 status report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-october-27-2026) says he took part in a full practice again and is probable to return against the Flyers. "Good sign," Carbery said, adding that the team would see how Thompson came out of practice and make a game plan for Wednesday. Until the Capitals name him, he stays unconfirmed on our board. Charlie Lindgren started Saturday's overtime win against Buffalo, per the [box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/buf-vs-wsh/2026/10/24/2026020173).

**Flyers: unconfirmed.** Wednesday is the second night of a back-to-back. Coach Rick Tocchet named Dan Vladar to start Tuesday against Columbus, [per PHLY Sports' Charlie O'Connor](https://x.com/charlieo_conn/status/2115088794047152486). Joseph Woll started Saturday against Anaheim ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/ana-vs-phi/2026/10/24/2026020168)). More on how the Flyers have split starts is in our [Vladar story](/articles/flyers-dan-vladar-starts-blue-jackets-capitals-back-to-back-oct-27-2026/).

## Flames at Oilers, 8:30 p.m. ET

Both teams played Monday. Devin Cooley made 26 saves in Calgary's 5-2 win over Toronto ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/tor-vs-cgy/2026/10/26/2026020186)), and Tristan Jarry made 26 saves in Edmonton's 4-3 shootout loss to Detroit ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/det-vs-edm/2026/10/26/2026020187)). Neither club has said who starts the Battle of Alberta. Edmonton is carrying Jarry and Matt Tomkins while Devon Levi is on injured reserve.

## Devils at Avalanche, 9 p.m. ET

**Devils: unconfirmed.** New Jersey plays at Dallas on Tuesday, so this is a back-to-back. Coach Sheldon Keefe confirmed Connor Hellebuyck makes his Devils debut in the Dallas game, [NHL.com reports](https://www.nhl.com/news/new-jersey-goalie-connor-hellebuyck-to-make-first-start-devils-debut-tonight-against-stars); the team has not said who starts in Colorado. Our [Hellebuyck debut story](/articles/devils-connor-hellebuyck-starts-debut-at-stars-oct-27-2026/) has more.

**Avalanche: unconfirmed.** Scott Wedgewood started Sunday's 6-2 loss at Nashville ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/col-vs-nsh/2026/10/25/2026020183)).

## Maple Leafs at Kraken, 10 p.m. ET

**Maple Leafs: unconfirmed.** Sergei Bobrovsky started Monday's loss at Calgary ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/tor-vs-cgy/2026/10/26/2026020186)), and Anthony Stolarz played Saturday at Edmonton.

**Kraken: unconfirmed.** Joey Daccord started Saturday's 5-1 loss to Minnesota ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/min-vs-sea/2026/10/24/2026020179)).
