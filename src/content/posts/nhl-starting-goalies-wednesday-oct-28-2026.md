[NHL.com's schedule](https://www.nhl.com/schedule/2026-10-28) has four games on Wednesday, Oct. 28. No team has named a starter yet. Below is the latest goalie news for each team and who started its most recent game, from official box scores. We will update the [Wednesday board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-28/) as starters are reported.

## Flyers at Capitals, 7:30 p.m. ET

**Capitals: unconfirmed.** Logan Thompson (lower body) took part in the first 40 minutes of Monday's practice, [NHL.com's status report says](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-october-26-2026). Coach Spencer Carbery called it a good step but would not say whether Thompson will be ready Wednesday. Charlie Lindgren started Saturday's overtime win against Buffalo, per the [box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/buf-vs-wsh/2026/10/24/2026020173).

**Flyers: unconfirmed.** Philadelphia hosts Columbus on Tuesday, so Wednesday is the second night of a back-to-back. Joseph Woll started Saturday against Anaheim ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/ana-vs-phi/2026/10/24/2026020168)).

## Flames at Oilers, 8:30 p.m. ET

Both teams play Monday night. Devin Cooley starts for Calgary against Toronto, and Tristan Jarry starts for Edmonton against Detroit (see our [Monday board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-26/)). Neither club has said who starts the Battle of Alberta. Edmonton is carrying Jarry and Matt Tomkins while Devon Levi is on injured reserve.

## Devils at Avalanche, 9 p.m. ET

**Devils: unconfirmed.** New Jersey plays at Dallas on Tuesday, so this is a back-to-back. Connor Hellebuyck practiced with the Devils for the first time Monday, and coach Sheldon Keefe said the plan is to get him involved on this road trip one day at a time, [NHL.com reports](https://www.nhl.com/news/connor-hellebuyck-says-he-is-ready-today-to-make-new-jersey-devils-debut). Our [Hellebuyck story](/articles/devils-connor-hellebuyck-ready-debut-stars-avalanche-oct-27-2026/) has more.

**Avalanche: unconfirmed.** Scott Wedgewood started Sunday's 6-2 loss at Nashville ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/col-vs-nsh/2026/10/25/2026020183)).

## Maple Leafs at Kraken, 10 p.m. ET

**Maple Leafs: unconfirmed.** Sergei Bobrovsky starts Monday at Calgary after Anthony Stolarz played Saturday at Edmonton.

**Kraken: unconfirmed.** Joey Daccord started Saturday's 5-1 loss to Minnesota ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/min-vs-sea/2026/10/24/2026020179)).
