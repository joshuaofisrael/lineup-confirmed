Elliotte Friedman reported on 5 November 2026 that New York Rangers goaltender James Reimer had cleared waivers.

Friedman's [X update](https://x.com/FriedgeHNIC/status/2118447126053077153) was brief: "Cleared" with the note "New: Reimer (NYR)" and an apology for the delay after a long drive. The post quoted an earlier Friedman item about other roster moves, but the clear news for Rangers depth is that Reimer went unclaimed.

## Corroboration on the waiver itself

[AP News](https://apnews.com/article/james-reimer-waivers-a8cd47b9c2868a00ea16659c9fb5d8cc) and [Pro Hockey Rumors](https://www.prohockeyrumors.com/2026/11/rangers-place-sean-durzi-on-ir-waive-james-reimer.html) reported that the Rangers had placed Reimer on waivers after he made only one start for them. Clearing means no other NHL club claimed him on that run, so New York can assign him to the AHL without another team taking his NHL rights off the wire.

For lineup and fantasy readers, the practical effect is a quieter Rangers crease picture: Reimer is not landing on another NHL roster via this waiver claim, and New York keeps control of where he plays next unless a separate trade follows.
