Two of Sunday's starters have been named a day early, and NHL.com's projections are in for the two afternoon games. Daylight saving time ends in the US overnight, so all times are Eastern Standard Time.

## Oilers at Rangers, 6 p.m. ET

**Oilers: Matt Tomkins, confirmed.** "Matt Tomkins is set to make his first start for the Oilers tomorrow night at the Rangers, per coach Mike Babcock," AP hockey writer [Stephen Whyno posted](https://x.com/SWhyno/status/2116636900731621680) after Saturday's game. It's the second night of a back-to-back for Edmonton, and Tristan Jarry gets the night off after making 18 saves in a [4-0 win at the Islanders](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/edm-vs-nyi/2026/10/31/2026020220), his second shutout in a row.

**Rangers: not announced.** James Reimer started Monday's 6-3 loss to Los Angeles and was relieved by Dylan Garand. [NHL.com's status report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-october-31-2026) says Joonas Korpisalo practiced Friday for the first time this regular season.

## Senators at Ducks, 8 p.m. ET

**Ducks: Laurent Brossoit, confirmed.** "Laurent Brossoit will start for the Anaheim Ducks tomorrow against the Ottawa Senators," Sporting Tribune Ducks reporter [Zach Cavanagh posted](https://x.com/ZachCav/status/2116608119149387974), adding that neither Lukas Dostal nor Ville Husso is injured. He quoted coach Joel Quenneville: "It's an opportunity."

**Senators: not announced.** Ottawa is also on a back-to-back. Samuel Ersson started Saturday's game at San Jose.

## NHL.com projections for the afternoon games

These are projections, not announcements, from NHL.com's projected lineups:

- **Jets at Hurricanes, 1 p.m. ET.** [Stuart Skinner for Winnipeg](https://www.nhl.com/news/winnipeg-jets-carolina-hurricanes-game-preview-november-1-2026), with Nico Daws second, and Pyotr Kochetkov for Carolina, with Brandon Bussi second. The Jets did not practice Saturday.
- **Wild at Capitals, 3:30 p.m. ET.** [Jesper Wallstedt for Minnesota](https://www.nhl.com/news/minnesota-wild-washington-capitals-game-preview-november-1-2026), with Calvin Pickard second, and Logan Thompson for Washington, with Charlie Lindgren second.
