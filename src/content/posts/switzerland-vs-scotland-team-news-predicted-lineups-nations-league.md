Switzerland host Scotland tonight, Monday 16 November, in the last round of Nations League Group B1. Kick-off at the Berit Sitterstadion in St Gallen is 7:45pm UK time (2:45pm ET), [according to the Scottish FA](https://www.scottishfa.co.uk/en/news/scotland-round-off-uefa-nations-league-campaign-with-swiss-test-in-st-gallen), and the match is live on BBC Two Scotland.

## What is at stake

Switzerland have already won the group. Scotland need three points and also need Slovenia to drop points away to North Macedonia to reach the promotion play-off spot, the Scottish FA says.

## Switzerland team news

[BBC Sport](https://www.bbc.com/sport/articles/c63rgqlqen7lo) reports that defender Manuel Akanji is out with a muscle issue, joining captain Granit Xhaka, midfielder Johan Manzambi and winger Dan Ndoye on the absentee list. Head coach Murat Yakin said he will "rotate a bit" while keeping experienced defenders, and confirmed that Sion striker Winsley Boteli, 20, and Young Boys midfielder Alvyn Sanches, 23, will both start. [FotMob](https://www.fotmob.com/matches/switzerland-vs-scotland/1wx88q) lists Boteli as doubtful with stomach trouble, so check the official team sheet about an hour before kick-off.

**Earlier predicted Switzerland XI (Sports Mole, before the latest absences):** Kobel; Athekame, Elvedi, Akanji, Britschgi; Freuler, Zakaria; Ndoye, Rieder, Amdouni; Embolo. Akanji and Ndoye are now ruled out, according to the BBC.

## Scotland team news

All Scotland players trained on Sunday and there are no fresh injury concerns, the Scottish FA says. Captain Andy Robertson is set for his 103rd cap, which would make him Scotland's most capped male player outright. FotMob lists Luis Binks (hamstring) as out and Stephen Welsh (muscle) as doubtful.

**Predicted Scotland XI (Sports Mole):** Gunn; Porteous, Graham, Tierney; Gannon-Doak, Ferguson, Gilmour, Robertson; McGinn, McTominay; Adams.

[Sports Mole](https://www.sportsmole.co.uk/football/switzerland/preview/switzerland-vs-scotland-prediction-team-news-lineups_609096.html) expects Sebastien Pocognoli to name the side that beat North Macedonia 2-1 on Friday, when John McGinn scored twice.

## Head to head

Switzerland won the reverse fixture 3-0 at Hampden. Across 18 meetings Scotland have won eight, drawn four and lost six, according to the Scottish FA.
