NHL.com published projected lineups on Friday for the six Saturday games that start before 5 p.m. ET. The goalie listed first is the projected starter. These are projections, not team announcements, so every goalie below is **expected** on the [Oct. 31 board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-31/). Teams usually confirm after Saturday's morning skate.

## Sabres at Kings (4 p.m. ET)

NHL.com's [Sabres at Kings projection](https://www.nhl.com/news/buffalo-sabres-los-angeles-kings-game-preview-october-31-2026) lists **Ukko-Pekka Luukkonen** first for Buffalo, with Colten Ellis second. Ellis started the last two games, including Thursday's 7-6 win at Anaheim. Luukkonen last played Oct. 24 against Washington, an overtime loss, and is 3-3-3 with a .866 save percentage in nine games, per his [NHL.com player page](https://www.nhl.com/player/ukko-pekka-luukkonen-8480045).

For Los Angeles, **Darcy Kuemper** is listed first, ahead of Anton Forsberg. NHL.com says Kuemper did not practice Friday for maintenance but is expected to play.

## Senators at Sharks (4 p.m. ET)

**Jonas Johansson** is listed first for Ottawa, with Samuel Ersson second, in the [Senators at Sharks projection](https://www.nhl.com/news/ottawa-senators-san-jose-sharks-game-preview-october-31-2026). Johansson started Thursday's 6-5 overtime loss at Los Angeles and was replaced after allowing four goals on 16 shots; Ersson made 20 saves in relief, the [box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/ott-vs-lak/2026/10/29/2026020212) shows. San Jose lists **Yaroslav Askarov** first, ahead of Alex Nedeljkovic. NHL.com notes Sharks coach Ryan Warsofsky did not reveal lineup decisions at Friday's optional practice.

## The four early games

- **Blackhawks at Bruins (1 p.m.):** Spencer Knight (Chicago) and Jeremy Swayman (Boston) are listed first, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/chicago-blackhawks-boston-bruins-game-preview-october-31-2026). Both started in losses on Thursday.
- **Blues at Red Wings (1 p.m.):** Joel Hofer (St. Louis) and John Gibson (Detroit) are listed first, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/st-louis-blues-detroit-red-wings-game-preview-october-31-2026). Both won on Thursday.
- **Lightning at Predators (2 p.m.):** Andrei Vasilevskiy (Tampa Bay) and Juuse Saros (Nashville) are listed first, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/tampa-bay-lightning-nashville-predators-game-preview-october-31-2026).
- **Oilers at Islanders (2 p.m.):** Tristan Jarry (Edmonton) and Ilya Sorokin (New York) are listed first, per [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/edmonton-oilers-new-york-islanders-game-preview-october-31-2026).

None of these teams plays on Friday, so no one in the afternoon games is on a back-to-back. The evening games, including Calgary's back-to-back at Colorado, are covered in our [Saturday slate preview](/articles/nhl-starting-goalies-saturday-oct-31-2026/). Nothing here is betting advice.
