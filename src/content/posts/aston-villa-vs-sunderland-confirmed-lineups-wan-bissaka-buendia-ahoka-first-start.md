The teams are in at Villa Park. "RLB's XI to take on Aston Villa!" [Sunderland posted on X](https://x.com/SunderlandAFC/status/2124137001553363346), and the headline name is a new one: "Jules Ahoka set for his first Premier League start," the club [added](https://x.com/SunderlandAFC/status/2124142034952884268).

Kick-off is 3pm UK (10am ET) on Saturday 21 November.

## Aston Villa confirmed XI

**Aston Villa:** Suzuki; Wan-Bissaka, Harwood-Bellis, Torres, Maatsen; Kamara, Goretzka, Alysson, Buendía, Manzambi; Jackson.

**Subs:** Bizot, Mings, Cissé, Barkley, McGinn, Mbaye, Gomes, Hemmings, Madjo.

Villa make two changes from their last game, against Manchester United, according to [the club](https://www.avfc.co.uk/news/2026/november/21/news-sunderland-team-news/). Aaron Wan-Bissaka returns from injury and Emi Buendía starts, with Victor Lindelöf and John McGinn making way. McGinn is on the bench, while Lindelöf is not in the squad. The [Sunderland Echo](https://www.sunderlandecho.com/sport/football/sunderland-afc/sunderland-team-news-aston-villa-team-news-9365511) says Taylor Harwood-Bellis moves to centre-back and Johan Manzambi starts despite a calf scare over the break. Villa are looking to make it seven league games unbeaten.

## Sunderland confirmed XI

**Sunderland:** Roefs; Meunier, Mukiele, O'Nien, Reinildo; Ahoka, Sadiki, Le Fée; Hume, Talbi, Isidor.

**Subs:** Alderete, Ellborg, Methalie, Danso, Rigg, Angulo, Tutierov, Browne, Fofana.

Régis Le Bris also makes two changes from the 1-0 win over Chelsea before the break, the Echo reports. Ahoka steps in for Granit Xhaka and Wilson Isidor replaces Brian Brobbey, who has a thigh problem. Chemsdine Talbi keeps his place for a second league start of the season, and Alan Browne is named in a Premier League squad for the first time this season.

**Out:** Granit Xhaka, Brian Brobbey, Dan Ballard, Romaine Mundle, Habib Diarra. Le Bris said Ballard and Mundle "should be back to train around mid-December".

## What to watch

Ahoka's debut start comes in the base of midfield, a big job away at a side unbeaten in six. For Villa, the fit-again Wan-Bissaka faces Sunderland's right side of Hume and Talbi.
