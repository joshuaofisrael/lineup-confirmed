The Boston Bruins placed rookie forward James Hagens on injured reserve on Saturday, 7 November, and recalled forward Mikey Eyssimont from Providence, [the club announced](https://www.nhl.com/bruins/news/bruins-place-james-hagens-on-ir-recall-mikey-eyssimont). The team listed 5 November as the date of injury.

## A few weeks for Hagens

Hagens left Thursday's win in Pittsburgh in the third period after taking a cross-check to the midsection from Penguins defenceman Samuel Girard, [BostonBruins.com reported](https://www.nhl.com/bruins/news/hagens-placed-on-ir-expected-to-miss-a-few-weeks). Head coach Marco Sturm said after Saturday's practice: "It's definitely gonna be a few weeks." He added that it was not a dirty play.

The 20-year-old, taken seventh overall in the 2025 draft, has six goals and six assists in 17 games this season.

## Eyssimont earns the call

Eyssimont, 30, leads all AHL skaters with 13 goals in eight games for Providence, including three hat tricks, and was named the AHL's Player of the Month for October. He played 56 games for Boston in 2025-26, scoring eight goals and 10 assists. Sturm said Eyssimont deserved the call-up after a strong training camp and a hot start in the AHL.

## Lines for Sunday

At Saturday's practice Marat Khusnutdinov took Hagens' spot on the right wing alongside Casey Mittelstadt and Elias Lindholm, with Matt Poitras moving to left wing next to Fraser Minten and Morgan Geekie. Sturm said final line decisions, including where Eyssimont fits, would be made before Sunday's matinee against Florida, and that the defence pairs will stay the same. The move does not affect Boston's goaltending; check game-day reports for the confirmed starter.
