Sergiño Dest will miss the United States' Concacaf Nations League quarter-final against Haiti. The national team [announced on X](https://x.com/USMNT/status/2120914160494285029) on Thursday that the defender would leave camp, and [US Soccer's release](https://www.ussoccer.com/stories/2026/11/usmnt/defender-sergino-dest-departs-november-training-camp) says he will go back to his club, PSV Eindhoven.

## What US Soccer said

According to US Soccer, Dest entered camp with a minor injury and, after a medical evaluation, will be kept out of the upcoming matches as a precaution. The federation gave no further medical detail. The roster now stands at 24 players.

[PSV says](https://www.psv.nl/en/media/artikel/nieuws-dest-uit-voorzorg-terug-naar-nederland) Dest left because of minor physical complaints and will continue preparing in the Netherlands for the club's next matches.

## The fixtures

- **First leg:** USA vs Haiti, Saturday 14 November, Nu Stadium, Miami, 4:30 p.m. ET (TNT, truTV, HBO Max, Telemundo, Universo, Peacock)
- **Second leg:** USA vs Haiti, Tuesday 17 November, TQL Stadium, Cincinnati, 7 p.m. ET

Both are home games for the US, per US Soccer.

## What it means for the line-up

Dest's absence removes a right-back option for the series. US Soccer has not named a replacement, so the 24 players already in camp are the pool for both legs. The confirmed starting XI is due from the federation closer to kick-off on Saturday.
