The Tampa Bay Lightning placed forward Brayden Point on injured reserve on Tuesday, according to the [NHL.com status report published on 11 November](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-november-11-2026). The league said Point left in the first period of a 4-3 loss to the Winnipeg Jets on Sunday with a lower-body injury. He has 16 points (five goals, 11 assists) in 17 games this season, NHL.com said.

The same report said the Lightning recalled forward Sam O'Reilly from Syracuse of the American Hockey League, and that O'Reilly "is expected to make his NHL debut against Buffalo Sabres on Wednesday (7 p.m. ET; HBO MAX, truTV, TNT)." That is the league's expected lineup note, not a confirmed post-game result.

Elsewhere in the same official report, NHL.com said Ottawa forward Nick Cousins will be out 7-10 days after leaving a 3-2 overtime loss to the Washington Capitals on Tuesday. Los Angeles's Kevin Fiala "is nearing his season debut, which could come as soon as Thursday against the New York Islanders," the league wrote; that wording is not a confirmation that he will play. Vegas coach Ryan Craig said Tuesday that Noah Hanifin will be out week to week with an undisclosed injury, and that Parker Wotherspoon (upper body) practiced in a regular jersey. Calgary coach Ryan Huska said Brennan Othmann will return against the New York Rangers on Wednesday after missing six games with an upper-body injury.

Those items are as published in the league's 11 November status report. This article does not add medical detail beyond that official account.
