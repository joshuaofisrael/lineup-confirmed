Mats Zuccarello returned to the Los Angeles Kings lineup against the Nashville Predators on Tuesday after missing six games with an upper-body injury, the [NHL status report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-november-10-2026) said on 11 November.

"We'll get him in and we'll keep an eye on him," Kings coach Peter Laviolette said. "He was (out) a little bit longer term, it wasn't just like a three or four day thing, so we'll keep an eye on him and his minutes. He'll probably start where he started in practice and then we'll see how it goes."

The same report said Kevin Fiala is nearing his season debut, which could come as soon as Thursday against the New York Islanders. Fiala sustained multiple fractures to his left lower leg while playing for Switzerland at the 2026 Winter Olympics in February and had a follow-up procedure in September. He has not played in the NHL this season.

Laviolette's comments treat Zuccarello's minutes as something to monitor after a longer absence, not as an automatic return to a full workload. Fiala's Thursday possibility is also not a confirmation; the report said the debut "could come as soon as Thursday."

The Islanders visit Los Angeles on Thursday at 10 p.m. Eastern time, the NHL report noted in its Islanders notes. Whether Fiala is in the lineup will be confirmed when the Kings complete their morning skate or post the official roster for that game.
