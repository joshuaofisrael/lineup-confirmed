The Winnipeg Jets activated defenseman Josh Morrissey from injured reserve and said he would return on Tuesday, 10 November, against the New Jersey Devils at Canada Life Centre, [NHL.com reported](https://www.nhl.com/news/winnipeg-jets-josh-morrissey-playing-status-update-november-10-2026) that afternoon.

Morrissey had missed 14 games with an upper-body injury sustained in the second period of a 3-2 win against the Colorado Avalanche on 7 October. He was initially listed as day to day. "It happened on kind of an unlucky play for me in that moment, and I was hoping it was not going to be as long as it was," Morrissey said after the morning skate, according to NHL.com. "But it happens, and I just worked hard to try and get back and be ready to go at my best as soon as possible."

He had three assists in four games this season and was averaging 20:04 of ice time. Last season he had 55 points (14 goals, 41 assists) in 77 games. Winnipeg was 4-8-2 without him in the lineup and entered the game at 7-8-3, while New Jersey was 13-5-0, NHL.com said.

"It is obviously a big boost for us having him back," Jets coach Scott Arniel said. "He's a big ice-time eater and he obviously plays against the other team's top line. Certainly, we know what he does with our (special) teams offensively. Going against a quick Jersey team with some real quick forwards, having him back is certainly helpful."

To open a roster spot, Winnipeg sent defenseman Henry Thrun to Manitoba of the American Hockey League, the report said. The NHL.com article was a pre-game status update and did not record whether Morrissey finished the match; lineup confirmation for later games will depend on subsequent club and league reports.
