Five games are on the [NHL schedule](https://www.nhl.com/schedule/2026-10-25) for Sunday. Here is where every crease stands as of Sunday morning ET. "Confirmed" means the team or an on-site reporter has said so; "expected" means a credible projection that is not yet official. The live, game-by-game version is on our [NHL starting goalies page](/nhl/starting-goalies/).

## Panthers at Blackhawks, 3 p.m. ET
- **Florida: Akira Schmid, confirmed.** The [Panthers' team preview](https://www.nhl.com/panthers/news/preview-panthers-try-to-pick-up-first-win-of-road-trip-in-chicago) says Schmid starts, with Jacob Markstrom out (upper body).
- **Chicago: Arvid Soderblom, expected.** [NHL.com's projection](https://www.nhl.com/news/florida-panthers-chicago-blackhawks-game-preview-october-25-2026) lists Soderblom first and says he could play after Spencer Knight started the past five games. More in our [Panthers-Blackhawks goalie report](/articles/panthers-akira-schmid-starts-at-blackhawks-oct-25-2026/).

## Islanders at Stars, 6 p.m. ET
- **New York: Semyon Varlamov, expected.** [NHL.com's projected lineup](https://www.nhl.com/news/new-york-islanders-dallas-stars-game-preview-october-25-2026) lists Varlamov first in goal, ahead of Ilya Sorokin. The Islanders have not confirmed it in sources we checked.
- **Dallas: Jake Oettinger, expected.** The [same NHL.com projection](https://www.nhl.com/news/new-york-islanders-dallas-stars-game-preview-october-25-2026) lists Oettinger with Casey DeSmith as backup.

## Canadiens at Jets (Heritage Classic), 7 p.m. ET
- **Both unconfirmed.** Winnipeg's options are Stuart Skinner and Clay Stevenson after the Hellebuyck trade, [NHL.com reports](https://www.nhl.com/news/winnipeg-jets-will-reset-after-connor-hellebuyck-traded-to-new-jersey-devils). See our [Heritage Classic goalie report](/articles/jets-goalie-heritage-classic-after-hellebuyck-trade-oct-25-2026/).

## Avalanche at Predators, 8 p.m. ET
- **Both unconfirmed.** We found no team or beat-reporter confirmation yet. Nashville is on the second night of a back-to-back, which often means a goalie change, but we will not list a starter until a reliable source does.

## Wild at Canucks, 10 p.m. ET
- **Both unconfirmed.** No confirmation yet from either team.
