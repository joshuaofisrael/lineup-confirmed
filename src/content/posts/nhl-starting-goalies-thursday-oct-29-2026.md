Thursday, Oct. 29 has 11 games on the [NHL schedule](https://www.nhl.com/schedule/2026-10-29). No team has reported a starter yet; morning-skate news arrives on game day. Most of these teams play Tuesday first, so the best guide for now is who starts for them tonight, listed on the [Oct. 27 board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-27/). Statuses for Thursday will move to expected or confirmed on the [Oct. 29 board](/nhl/starting-goalies/2026-10-29/) as they are reported.

## Three teams to watch

**Capitals (at Nashville, 8 p.m. ET): back-to-back.** Washington hosts Philadelphia on Wednesday and plays in Nashville on Thursday. NHL.com's [status report](https://www.nhl.com/news/nhl-status-report-news-and-notes-october-27-2026) lists Logan Thompson (lower body) as probable to return Wednesday. Charlie Lindgren started Saturday's win against Buffalo. Who plays which night has not been announced.

**Red Wings (vs Chicago, 7:30 p.m. ET): Postava or Gibson?** Michal Postava won his first NHL start Monday at Edmonton, with John Gibson as backup, [NHL.com reports](https://www.nhl.com/news/detroit-red-wings-edmonton-oilers-game-recap-october-26-2026). Detroit has not said who starts its first game back home. Background: our [Postava story](/articles/red-wings-michal-postava-wins-first-nhl-start-shootout-oilers-oct-27-2026/).

**Lightning and Penguins: rested.** Neither team plays Tuesday or Wednesday. Dennis Hildeby started Tampa Bay's last game, Saturday at Utah ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/tbl-vs-uta/2026/10/24/2026020177)), and Arturs Silovs started Pittsburgh's 3-2 overtime win over Nashville the same night ([box score](https://www.nhl.com/gamecenter/nsh-vs-pit/2026/10/24/2026020172)).
