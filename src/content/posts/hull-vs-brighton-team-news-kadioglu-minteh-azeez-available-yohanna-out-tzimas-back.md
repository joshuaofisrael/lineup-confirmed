Brighton head to Hull with most of their squad back. "Brighton's Fabian Hurzeler discusses his side's fitness ahead of their Premier League encounter with Hull on Sunday," [Sky Sports News posted on X](https://x.com/SkySportsNews/status/2123715826805047612) on Friday morning, with the head coach's update.

Hull City vs Brighton kicks off at 2pm UK time (9am ET) on Sunday 22 November at the MKM Stadium.

## Brighton team news

- **Available:** Ferdi Kadioglu, Yankuba Minteh, Chema Andres, Femi Azeez and Pascal Struijk, [BBC Sport](https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/articles/cmy0ed5kjg06o) reports from Hurzeler's news conference. The [club's own report](https://www.brightonandhovealbion.com/media-article/hull-city-team-news-chema-minteh-kadioglu-and-azzez-in-contention) confirms Kadioglu, Chema, Minteh and Azeez are all available for the trip.
- **Zadok Yohanna: out.** He was injured again while with Nigeria's under-21s. "Unfortunately, it wasn't perfectly managed and therefore he had a re-injury. It was very bad and very unlucky for Zadok," Hurzeler said, according to the club.
- **Stefanos Tzimas: back in full training, not yet an option.** It is his first full training in nearly 11 months after a knee injury. "It won't be that he will immediately be an option for us. He was out for a long time, so we need to give him time," Hurzeler said.
- **Pascal Gross** could make his 300th appearance for the club on Sunday, BBC Sport says.

## Selection hint

Hurzeler warned against reading too much into who is left out. BBC Sport says he told reporters that a player missing from the squad "doesn't mean they're out", only that he has a lot of players to choose from. With five players back, expect some names to miss the matchday squad for that reason.

## Hull

Hurzeler expects a patient game against a deep, organised side. "They are very structured. Arsenal suffered so we need to be patient," he said, according to BBC Sport. "We know they are good in transition and have individual quality, as well as set piece."

We will publish the confirmed lineups when the teams are announced, about an hour before kick-off.
