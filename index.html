New Jersey Devils at Vegas Golden Knights 6 p.m. ET

  • Devils: Connor Hellebuyck Expected
    NHL.com says the Devils are likely to go back to Hellebuyck, who made 17 saves in a 5-1 win at Dallas on Tuesday, for what would be his second start for New Jersey. Jake Allen started Wednesday at Colorado. Last game of a three-game trip. Source: NHL.com (NHL On Tap, Oct. 30).
  • Golden Knights: Adin Hill Expected
    Listed first in goal, with Cameron Whitehead second, in NHL.com's projected lineups. The same preview says Carter Hart (undisclosed) has skated but his status is unclear. Source: NHL.com (Devils at Golden Knights preview).

Winnipeg Jets at Florida Panthers 7 p.m. ET

  • Jets: Nico Daws Expected
    NHL.com's preview lists Daws first in goal and says he could start; the Jets did not hold a morning skate. Second night of a back-to-back after Thursday's 6-4 loss at Tampa Bay, where Daws made nine saves in relief of Stuart Skinner. Source: NHL.com (Jets at Panthers preview).
  • Panthers: Akira Schmid Confirmed
    "Akira Schmid will be in net tonight against Winnipeg, per Coach Maurice," the Panthers posted on game day. NHL.com's preview says Jacob Markstrom (upper body) has missed four straight games and could return sometime next week. Source: Florida Panthers on X.

Seattle Kraken at Calgary Flames 8 p.m. ET

  • Kraken: Philipp Grubauer Expected
    Should start for a second straight game based on the morning skate, the Kraken's Geoff Baker wrote on the team site; NHL.com's projected lineups also list Grubauer first, with Joey Daccord second. Not a coach announcement. Grubauer started Wednesday's 5-4 win against Toronto. Source: Seattle Kraken (game preview, Oct. 30).
  • Flames: Dustin Wolf Confirmed
    "Dustin Wolf starting tonight vs. Seattle," Flames radio's Pat Steinberg posted, his first start since Oct. 15 at Vegas. Devin Cooley left Wednesday's game at Edmonton with a lower-body injury, and the Flames recalled goalie Arsenii Sergeev from AHL Calgary on Friday. Source: Pat Steinberg (Flames radio) on X.
