    Started, as the team preview said he would. Jacob Markstrom is injured (upper body). Source: NHL.com GameCenter (Panthers at Blackhawks, Oct. 25).

New York Islanders at Dallas Stars 6 p.m. ET

  • Islanders: Ilya Sorokin Expected
    The Islanders' own game preview says Sorokin is expected to start, though unconfirmed. (NHL.com's earlier projection had listed Semyon Varlamov first.) Source: NewYorkIslanders.com (Shannon Bickert).

Montreal Canadiens at Winnipeg Jets (Heritage Classic) 7 p.m. ET

  • Canadiens: Jacob Fowler Expected
    Listed first in goal in NHL.com's projected lineup, ahead of Jakub Dobes. Fowler also started Friday's 4-1 win at Chicago. Sam Montembeault is injured. Source: NHL.com projected lineups.
  • Jets: Stuart Skinner Expected
    Listed first in goal in NHL.com's projected lineup, with Clay Stevenson as backup and Nico Daws a scratch. First game since the Connor Hellebuyck trade. Source: NHL.com projected lineups.

Colorado Avalanche at Nashville Predators 8 p.m. ET

  • Avalanche: Scott Wedgewood Expected
    Listed first in goal in NHL.com's projected lineup, with Mackenzie Blackwood (Friday's starter) as backup. Source: NHL.com projected lineups.
  • Predators: Justus Annunen Expected
    NHL.com: Annunen is expected to start after Juuse Saros made 34 saves in Saturday's overtime loss at Pittsburgh. Source: NHL.com projected lineups.

Minnesota Wild at Vancouver Canucks 10 p.m. ET

  • Wild: Calvin Pickard Confirmed
    Coach John Hynes set the back-to-back plan: Jesper Wallstedt started Saturday at Seattle, Pickard starts Sunday. Source: The Hockey News (Wild).
  • Canucks: Leevi Merilainen Expected
    Projected to start after Kevin Lankinen played Thursday; listed first in NHL.com's projected lineup. Source: The Hockey News (Canucks).
