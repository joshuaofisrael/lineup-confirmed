Los Angeles Kings at New York Rangers 7 p.m. ET

  • Kings: Darcy Kuemper Expected
    LA Kings Insider's game-day preview says to look for Kuemper back in net after Anton Forsberg started Saturday, and NHL.com's projected lineup lists him first. We have not seen a team announcement, so he stays expected. Source: LA Kings Insider (Oct. 26 preview).

    NHL.com's status report says Reimer will make his first start for the Rangers, a day after signing a one-year contract. Dylan Garand is the backup. Source: NHL.com (Kings at Rangers projected lineups).

    NHL.com: Bobrovsky will start after Anthony Stolarz started Saturday's 7-2 win at Edmonton. Source: NHL.com (Maple Leafs at Flames projected lineups).

Detroit Red Wings at Edmonton Oilers 9:30 p.m. ET

  • Red Wings: Michal Postava Confirmed
    Coach Todd McLellan announced after the morning skate that Postava will make his first NHL start. John Gibson is the backup; NHL.com lists Daniil Tarasov as injured. Source: MLive (Ansar Khan, Oct. 26).
  • Oilers: Tristan Jarry Confirmed
    The Oilers' official X account posted that Jarry starts against Detroit. Matt Tomkins is the backup with Devon Levi on injured reserve. Source: Edmonton Oilers on X (Oct. 26).
