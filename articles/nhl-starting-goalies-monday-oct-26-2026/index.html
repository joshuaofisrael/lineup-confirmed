NHL Starting Goalies Monday, Oct. 26, 2026: Kings-Rangers, Leafs-Flames, Red Wings-Oilers

By Staff Desk

Monday's three-game NHL slate has no confirmed starters yet. Here is who started each team's last game, from official NHL box scores, so you know what to watch for.

Last updated October 25, 2026

Monday, Oct. 26 brings three games on the NHL schedule, and as of Sunday afternoon no team has named its starter. All six teams played on Saturday, so nobody is on the second night of a back-to-back. Below is who started each club's most recent game, taken from the official NHL box scores. We will mark starters confirmed on the Monday board as teams announce them.

Los Angeles Kings at New York Rangers, 7 p.m. ET

Kings: unconfirmed. Anton Forsberg started Saturday's 3-1 loss at New Jersey and made 23 saves on 25 shots, per NHL.com. Darcy Kuemper started the game before that, Thursday at the Islanders.

Rangers: unconfirmed. Dylan Garand started Saturday's 7-4 loss at Ottawa, stopping 17 of 22 shots, per NHL.com.

Toronto Maple Leafs at Calgary Flames, 8:30 p.m. ET

Maple Leafs: unconfirmed. Anthony Stolarz started Saturday's 7-2 win at Edmonton with 30 saves on 32 shots, per NHL.com.

Flames: unconfirmed. Devin Cooley started Saturday's 4-1 win over Detroit and made 28 saves on 29 shots, per NHL.com.

Detroit Red Wings at Edmonton Oilers, 9:30 p.m. ET

Red Wings: unconfirmed. John Gibson started Saturday's loss at Calgary, making 21 saves on 25 shots, per NHL.com.

Oilers: unconfirmed. Tristan Jarry started Saturday's 7-2 loss to Toronto with 25 saves on 30 shots, per NHL.com.

Teams usually confirm starters after the morning skate on game day. For Sunday's games, see tonight's NHL starting goalies. Nothing here is betting advice.
