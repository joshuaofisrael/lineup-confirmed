NHL: Connor McDavid hits 18-game season-opening point streak and ties Adam Oates assist run

By Staff Desk

Connor McDavid has a point in all 18 games to start 2026-27, making him the sixth player in NHL history with a season-opening run that long, and his 18-game assist streak ties Adam Oates for the second longest, behind only Wayne Gretzky's 23, per NHL Public Relations.

Last updated November 8, 2026

Connor McDavid has recorded a point in each of the Edmonton Oilers' first 18 games of the 2026-27 season, NHL Public Relations said on Saturday night, 7 November, after Edmonton played the Vancouver Canucks.

In a post on X, the league's PR account said McDavid is the sixth player in NHL history to record "a season-opening run of at least that length."

Chasing Gretzky's assist mark

All 18 games have included an assist. NHL Public Relations said the streak ties Adam Oates, who had an 18-game run in 1992-93, for the second-longest assist streak in NHL history. Only Wayne Gretzky, with 23 straight games in 1990-91, has gone longer. McDavid needs five more games with an assist to match Gretzky.

Lineup note

The posts are milestone notes, not a lineup or injury update. For Edmonton's next starting goalie and line combinations, wait for the team's game-day report.

Primary sources: the NHL Public Relations point-streak post and assist-streak post.
