Haiti vs USMNT team news: predicted lineups for today's Nations League quarter-final in Miami

By Staff Desk

The first leg kicks off at 4:30 p.m. ET at Nu Stadium. Pulisic, Balogun and McKennie are back, Dest, Campbell and Reyna are out, and it is one of Pochettino's last two games in charge.

Last updated November 14, 2026

The United States start their Concacaf Nations League quarter-final against Haiti today, Saturday 14 November, at Nu Stadium in Miami. US Soccer lists kickoff at 4:30 p.m. ET on TNT, truTV, HBO Max, Telemundo, Universo and Peacock. The second leg is on 17 November at TQL Stadium in Cincinnati at 7 p.m. ET.

USMNT team news

US Soccer has named a 24-man squad: three goalkeepers, eight defenders, eight midfielders and five forwards. Sports Illustrated reports that Sergiño Dest is the latest to withdraw through injury, after George Campbell and Gio Reyna pulled out and were replaced by Mark McKenzie and Jack McGlynn. We covered Dest's departure from camp on Friday.

Christian Pulisic, Folarin Balogun and Weston McKennie are back after missing the previous window, SI says.

This tie is also the end of an era. US Soccer says Mauricio Pochettino will leave after the two legs against Haiti to become head coach of Manchester United.

Predicted lineups

These are SI's predictions, not confirmed team sheets. Official lineups are usually released about an hour before kickoff.

USMNT (4-2-3-1): Kochen; Freeman, Richards, Trusty, Robinson; Adams, McKennie; Sullivan, Tillman, Pulisic; Balogun.

Haiti (4-2-3-1): Pierre; Arcus, Duverne, Delcroix, Expérience; Jacques, Sainté; Deedson, Picault, Providence; Isidor.

Haiti team news

SI reports that Jean-Ricner Bellegarde is missing through injury. It says Alexander Pierre has taken over in goal from the retired Johny Placide, and that Sunderland's Wilson Isidor is expected to lead the attack.
