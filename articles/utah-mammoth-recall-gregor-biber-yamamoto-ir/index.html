Utah Mammoth recall Gregor Biber, place Kailer Yamamoto on IR

By Staff Desk

The Utah Mammoth recalled defenseman Gregor Biber from AHL Tucson and placed winger Kailer Yamamoto on injured reserve after Nate Schmidt’s upper-body injury.

Last updated November 7, 2026

The Utah Mammoth recalled defenseman Gregor Biber from the AHL’s Tucson Roadrunners and placed forward Kailer Yamamoto on injured reserve, PuckBeat reported on 7 November 2026.

Why the move happened

PuckBeat said the transactions followed an upper-body injury to defenseman Nate Schmidt in Thursday’s game in Montreal. Biber is expected to serve as a seventh defenseman behind Artem Duda until Schmidt recovers. Pro Hockey Rumors carried the same recall-and-IR pairing.

Biber, a 2024 fourth-round pick (98th overall), is the first player from that Utah draft class to earn an NHL recall, according to PuckBeat. The 20-year-old Austrian spent the previous two seasons with Rögle in Sweden’s SHL, recording three goals and two assists in 88 games, and signed a three-year entry-level contract with the Mammoth in May. In 11 games with Tucson this season he has three assists and 10 penalty minutes.

Yamamoto’s IR placement

Yamamoto, 28, had been out with an upper-body injury for more than a week after an initial day-to-day designation. PuckBeat said the IR placement is retroactive to 29 October, which makes him eligible to return when medically cleared. Before the injury he had four goals and three assists in nine games.

Elite Prospects lists Biber’s recall/reassignment to Utah dated 7 November 2026, aligning with the beat reporting.

Lineup takeaway

For fantasy and lineup desks, the immediate roster effect is a depth defenseman arriving from Tucson while Yamamoto’s IR clock is formalised. Schmidt’s absence is the injury driver; Biber’s recall is organisational depth rather than a guaranteed starting-six lock until the coaching staff shows the pairings in warmups. Watch Tucson’s next blueline combinations for who replaces Biber in the AHL, and watch Utah’s scratch list for whether Biber dresses as 7D or stays in street clothes while Schmidt’s status firms up.
