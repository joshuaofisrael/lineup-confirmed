Premier League Lineups November 20, 2026

Arsenal lose Zubimendi for “a few weeks” before Newcastle, but Saliba is back and ready

By Staff Desk

Mikel Arteta says Martin Zubimendi is out for a few weeks with a hamstring injury picked up for Spain, and Bukayo Saka is about halfway through an eight-week lay-off. William Saliba is available for Saturday's trip to Newcastle (5:30pm UK, 12:30pm ET).

Last updated November 20, 2026

Arsenal's midfield takes a hit before one of their toughest away trips. "Arsenal lose Martin Zubimendi as he's suffered an injury," Fabrizio Romano posted on X on Friday morning, quoting Mikel Arteta: "Zubi is out with a slight hamstring issue. A few weeks out."

Newcastle vs Arsenal kicks off at 5:30pm UK time (12:30pm ET) on Saturday 21 November at St James' Park. This update replaces the early injury picture in our Thursday preview.

Arsenal team news

  • Martin Zubimendi (hamstring): out for a few weeks. He went off in Spain's 3-2 defeat by England. "He wasn't too concerned to be fair after the game, but after the MRI scans, it showed that there is an injury there and he's going to be out for a few weeks," Arteta said, according to Arsenal.com.
  • Bukayo Saka (muscle): out. "It looks like it's going to be an eight-week injury more or less. I think he's at four at the moment," Arteta said. BBC Sport describes it as a quadriceps injury.
  • William Saliba: available. "He's ready, he's in good condition. We want to ramp up his minutes," Arteta said. Asked if Saliba is available for selection, he answered "Yes", according to the club's full transcript.
  • Gabriel: back from Brazil duty in Singapore and "in good condition", Arteta said. The club says there are no other specific injury concerns.

What it means for the lineup

Zubimendi has been the base of Arsenal's midfield, so Arteta has to rethink the shape in front of the defence. He did not say who will replace him, and we will not guess at a name. Saliba's return gives Arteta a strong option at centre-back, though the manager's talk of ramping up minutes suggests he may be managed carefully.

Newcastle

Arteta expects a hard game against Newcastle's new manager. BBC Sport quotes him naming Matthias Jaissle and saying Bruno Guimaraes "knows the side really well". Our Thursday preview has the early Newcastle injury list.

We will publish the confirmed lineups when the teams are announced, about an hour before kick-off.
