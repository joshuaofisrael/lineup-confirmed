Premier League Lineups November 17, 2026

Wales vs Portugal team news: Ampadu suspended, Neco Williams out, Portugal without Dias, Palhinha and Bernardo Silva

By Staff Desk

Wales host group leaders Portugal at Cardiff City Stadium tonight. Connor Roberts is expected at right-back and Josh Sheehan in midfield, while Portugal need only a draw to finish top of Group A4.

Last updated November 17, 2026

Wales host Portugal at Cardiff City Stadium tonight, Tuesday 17 November, in their last Nations League Group A4 match. The game is live in the UK on BBC One Wales, BBC Two and S4C, according to a preview carried by Yahoo Sports.

Wales team news

Right-back Neco Williams is ruled out after an injury in Oslo, and midfielder Ethan Ampadu serves a one-match suspension. Goalkeeper Karl Darlow missed Monday's training and faces a late fitness check. Centre-back Ben Cabango and winger Sorba Thomas are back after serving one-match bans.

  • Premier League Lineups November 16, 2026

    Kick-off is 7:45pm UK time (2:45pm ET) in St Gallen. Switzerland have already won Group B1 and will make changes; Scotland report no fresh injuries and need a win plus help from North Macedonia.

Connor Roberts is expected to step in at right-back and Josh Sheehan to anchor the midfield. David Brooks, who scored off the bench against Norway, and Rubin Colwill are pushing for starts, the preview says.

Predicted Wales lineup

Darlow; Roberts, Rodon, Davies, Dasilva; Cullen, Sheehan; Johnson, Brooks, Wilson; Koumas. Darlow's place depends on his fitness test.

Portugal team news

Jorge Jesus is without defender Ruben Dias and midfielder Joao Palhinha, who are both suspended. Captain Bernardo Silva did not travel to Cardiff after a knock at the weekend, and left-back Nuno Mendes is injured. Joao Cancelo returns from suspension and Carlos Forbs is pushing for a start after impressing off the bench on Saturday. Portugal are also adjusting to life without Cristiano Ronaldo after his international retirement earlier this month.

The same preview lists Bernardo Silva in its predicted Portugal XI despite reporting that he did not travel, so we have not repeated that lineup here.

Form

Wales are bottom of the group after losing four of five games, including Saturday's 3-1 defeat in Norway. Portugal lost 4-1 at home to Denmark on Saturday but still top the group and need just a draw to finish first.
