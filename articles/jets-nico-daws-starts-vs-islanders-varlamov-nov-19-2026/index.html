NHL Starting Goalies November 19, 2026

Nico Daws is in the Jets' starter's net for tonight, and Semyon Varlamov looks set to start for the Islanders

By Staff Desk

Nico Daws took the starter's net at Winnipeg's morning skate and is expected to face the New York Islanders on Thursday, 7pm ET. Semyon Varlamov was in the Islanders' starter's crease. Neither team had confirmed by early afternoon.

Last updated November 19, 2026

The Winnipeg Jets look set to go back to Nico Daws. "Greetings from #NHLJets morning skate, where Nico Daws is in the starter's net and looks set to be in goal tonight against the New York Islanders," Winnipeg reporter Mike McIntyre posted on X on Thursday morning. Connor Hrabchak posted the same from the rink.

The Islanders play at the Jets at 7pm ET (6pm local). Both starters are listed as likely, not confirmed, so check back before puck drop.

Jets: Nico Daws (likely)

Daily Faceoff lists Daws as Winnipeg's likely starter, citing Mitchell Clinton. It says Daws has a 2.32 goals against average and a .913 save percentage in four appearances for the Jets (2-1-0) since his trade from New Jersey, and stopped 12 of 13 shots in relief in last week's shootout win over Chicago. His season numbers are 4-3-0 with a .892 save percentage. We covered his first Jets start in October.

Islanders: Semyon Varlamov (likely)

Daily Faceoff, citing Andrew Gross, says Varlamov was in the starter's crease at the Islanders' morning skate. The 38-year-old is 2-2-2 this season with a 2.60 goals against average and a .898 save percentage, and made 26 saves on 28 shots in his last outing, a 6-2 win over Anaheim.

Form

Winnipeg's six-game winning streak ended on Tuesday in a 2-1 loss to Ottawa. The Islanders are 3-0-0 in their past three games and have outscored opponents 16-5 in that run, according to Daily Faceoff.
