Flyers GM Briere says report that Michkov wants out is 'not correct'

By Staff Desk

Danny Briere said Matvei Michkov and his agent told the Flyers they were not the source of a report that the forward wants a fresh start elsewhere. Michkov said he is happy in Philadelphia.

Last updated November 12, 2026

Philadelphia Flyers general manager Danny Briere said Wednesday that a report saying Matvei Michkov wants to play elsewhere "was not correct," NHL.com reported.

On Tuesday, NHL Network and ESPN analyst Kevin Weekes, citing league sources, said the 21-year-old forward likely prefers a fresh start elsewhere, according to NHL.com.

What the Flyers said

Briere said he met Michkov in Edmonton on Wednesday morning and the player told him the report did not come from him. Briere also spoke with Michkov's agent, Paul Theofanous, who said the same, NBC Sports Philadelphia reported. Coach Rick Tocchet also rejected the report, per NHL.com.

Michkov said he is happy playing for the Flyers and was focused on the game. Asked whether he was happy in Philadelphia, he said yes, per NHL.com.

Both Michkov and defenseman Nick Seeler played down a heated exchange on the bench during Monday's 3-1 win at Vancouver. Briere said the team's frustration comes from its start to the season, not a split with Michkov.

Lineup status

Michkov played in Wednesday's 3-2 overtime loss at Edmonton, and nothing from the club suggests a change to his role. He has 15 points (10 goals, five assists) in 21 games, which leads the Flyers, according to NHL.com. He can become a restricted free agent after the season. Briere said he does not discuss contracts publicly, per NBC Sports Philadelphia.

Philadelphia entered Wednesday at 5-10-5, six months after reaching the second round of the playoffs.
