France vs Türkiye team news: Mbappé available, Upamecano out, and predicted lineups in Bordeaux

By Staff Desk

Zidane says Mbappé can return after missing the 3-1 defeat to Italy. Upamecano has left the squad for personal reasons, and already-relegated Türkiye are without the suspended Ozan Kabak.

Last updated November 15, 2026

France finish their Nations League group against Türkiye at the Matmut Atlantique in Bordeaux tonight, Sunday 15 November, and Kylian Mbappé is available again. Zinedine Zidane confirmed on Saturday that his captain could return after missing Thursday's 3-1 defeat to Italy with a minor muscular problem, Managing Madrid reports. Zidane would not say whether Mbappé would play centrally or wide.

France team news

Dayot Upamecano has withdrawn from the squad for personal reasons, and Ibrahima Konaté is expected to replace him, Get French Football News reports. Wolfsburg's Sael Kumbedi could start at right-back. The defeat in Milan was Zidane's first as France coach.

Türkiye team news

Türkiye are bottom of Group A1 with no points from five games and were relegated to League B after Thursday's 2-1 home defeat to Belgium, their first match under new head coach Sergen Yalçın, Yahoo Sports says. Centre-back Ozan Kabak is suspended, with Merih Demiral likely to come in. Hakan Çalhanoğlu and Arda Güler are expected to start.

Predicted Türkiye XI (Yahoo Sports): Çakır; Aydın, Bardakcı, Demiral, Kadıoğlu; Yüksek, Kökçü; Akgün, Güler, Yılmaz; Ünal.
