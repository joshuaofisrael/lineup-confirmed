Manchester United Name Mbeumo and Cunha Against Roma in Champions League XI

By Staff Desk

Analysis

Manchester United’s confirmed Old Trafford lineup features Bryan Mbeumo up front and Matheus Cunha behind him, while Roma start Donyell Malen.

Last updated November 3, 2026

Manchester United and Roma have named their starting teams for their November 3 Champions League meeting at Old Trafford, with kick-off scheduled for 20:00 UK time. The confirmed selections, published by Oddschecker at 18:52, show United in a 4-2-3-1 and Roma in a 3-4-2-1. These are announced lineups, not pre-match projections.

United start Senne Lammens in goal, with Diogo Dalot, Harry Maguire, Ayden Heaven and Leny Yoro listed across the back. Carlos Baleba and Kobbie Mainoo occupy the midfield positions behind Bruno Fernandes, Amad Diallo and Matheus Cunha, while Bryan Mbeumo is the named central forward. The configuration puts significant creative responsibility on Fernandes and Cunha, with Amad offering a different route into the final third.

The combination of Baleba and Mainoo is one of the more revealing decisions. United are not simply selecting a forward-heavy eleven and hoping to dominate possession. Their two central midfielders will have to manage Roma’s attempts to move the ball through a compact central structure, while also supplying Fernandes in advanced positions. If either midfielder is drawn too far forward, the space behind United’s attack could become a transition route for Roma.

Roma’s announced team begins with Devis Vásquez in goal and a back three of Gianluca Mancini, Mario Hermoso and Leonardo Balerdi. Bryan Cristante, Wesley, Marten de Roon and Devyne Rensch form the listed midfield band, with Matías Soulé and Rodrigo Mora supporting Donyell Malen. The three-defender system creates a tactical contrast with United’s four-player back line and offers Roma options to build with an extra central defender.

The visitors’ selection suggests particular attention should fall on the areas outside United’s midfield pair. Soulé and Mora can receive between the lines or drift toward the channels, potentially drawing a defender out and creating space for Malen. United’s full-backs, meanwhile, will have to judge when to step forward and when to protect the spaces that Roma’s wide players may attack. These are tactical possibilities inferred from the lineup, not claims about how the match has unfolded.

United’s bench includes Joshua Zirkzee, Noussair Mazraoui, Lisandro Martínez, Matthijs de Ligt, Mason Mount, Youri Tielemans and Shea Lacey, among others. Those options provide different ways to adjust the shape: another striker, additional defensive cover or fresh attacking midfield personnel. None of those players should be described as starters on the basis of this team sheet.

Roma list Nahuel Molina, Santiago Castro, Daniele Ghilardi, Evan Ndicka, Konstantinos Koulierakis and Niccolò Pisilli among their substitutes. With the match beginning at 20:00, the confirmed benches matter because managers may need to respond quickly to how the opposing system presses and creates overloads.

The fixture is part of the fourth matchday of the 2026–27 Champions League league phase. UEFA’s published calendar places United–Roma among Tuesday’s nine fixtures, with Wednesday bringing a separate slate including Fenerbahçe–Liverpool. It is important not to confuse the two days when presenting confirmed lineups: Wednesday’s teams had not been announced at the same time as Tuesday’s Old Trafford selections.

For readers checking just before kickoff, the central news is straightforward. Mbeumo leads United’s attack, Cunha and Fernandes start behind him, and Roma counter with Malen supported by Soulé and Mora. Any later change, withdrawal or tactical reshuffle would require an updated official team sheet rather than speculation.
