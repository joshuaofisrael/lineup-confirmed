Premier League Lineups November 20, 2026

Pochettino says he will pick his first Man Utd team “tomorrow”, and Maguire and Dorgu have both trained

By Staff Desk

In his first news conference as Manchester United head coach, Mauricio Pochettino confirmed Bruno Fernandes stays captain and said he will decide the squad for Liverpool on Saturday. Harry Maguire and Patrick Dorgu completed full training on Thursday; Luke Shaw is a doubt.

Last updated November 20, 2026

Mauricio Pochettino has made his first public appearance as Manchester United head coach. "Mauricio Pochettino will face the media for the first time as Manchester United head coach and you can watch it LIVE on Sky Sports News YouTube from 2pm," Sky Sports News posted on X on Friday morning.

Liverpool vs Man Utd kicks off at 4:30pm UK time (11:30am ET) on Sunday 22 November at Anfield. Our Thursday preview has the full early injury list for both sides.

What Pochettino said about selection

He has not named a team yet. "I know as head coach I am the guy who tomorrow will decide the squad for Sunday, pick the 11, make the changes," he said, according to The Telegraph.

On the captaincy he was clear. "Bruno is our captain and a very important player but all the players need to feel they are important," he said. The Telegraph adds that he expects to discuss Fernandes's contract extension after Sunday.

He also said he spent three hours on Thursday in one-to-one talks with players, but "I think I missed some injured players", according to NBC Sports.

Man Utd team news

  • Harry Maguire (back) and Patrick Dorgu (hamstring): trained. Both took part in Thursday's full session, which United confirmed, the Evening Standard reports. Pochettino has not yet confirmed either for Sunday.
  • Lisandro Martinez: available. His suspension for the red card at Stamford Bridge has been served, the Standard says.
  • Luke Shaw: doubt. NBC Sports lists him as a doubt.
  • Marcus Rashford: pushing to return. NBC Sports describes a groin injury; the Standard refers to a hip problem. Neither says he is confirmed fit.
  • Karl Darlow and Ayden Heaven: question marks, according to NBC Sports.

What it means

Pochettino has had only a few days with the squad, and he says the focus is "100% on Liverpool". Getting Maguire and Dorgu back helps a defence that conceded four against Aston Villa last time out. He did not say whether he will change the system or keep the team that drew that game.

We will publish the confirmed lineups when the teams are announced, about an hour before kick-off.
