Premier League Lineups November 19, 2026

Newcastle vs Arsenal team news: Saka still out, a Zubimendi hamstring scare and Joelinton set to miss St James' Park

By Staff Desk

Kick-off is 5:30pm UK time (12:30pm ET) on Saturday 21 November. Bukayo Saka is expected out until December, Max Dowman serves the last game of his ban and Martin Zubimendi limped off for Spain. Newcastle look set to be without Joelinton and Ewen Jaouen.

Last updated November 19, 2026

Newcastle United host Arsenal at St James' Park on Saturday 21 November, the first Premier League match for both clubs after the international break. Kick-off is 5:30pm UK time (12:30pm ET), live on Sky Sports, according to the Premier League's November fixture list.

This is early team news, compiled before Thursday and Friday's press conferences. We will publish the confirmed lineups when the teams are announced.

    Arsenal lose Zubimendi for “a few weeks” before Newcastle, but Saliba is back and ready

    Mikel Arteta says Martin Zubimendi is out for a few weeks with a hamstring injury picked up for Spain, and Bukayo Saka is about halfway through an eight-week lay-off. William Saliba is available for Saturday's trip to Newcastle (5:30pm UK, 12:30pm ET).

  • Premier League Lineups November 20, 2026

    Fabian Hurzeler says Ferdi Kadioglu, Yankuba Minteh, Chema Andres, Femi Azeez and Pascal Struijk are available for Sunday's trip to Hull (2pm UK, 9am ET). Zadok Yohanna is injured again after Nigeria under-21 duty, and Stefanos Tzimas is back training but not yet an option.

  • Premier League Lineups November 19, 2026

    Liverpool vs Man Utd team news: Pochettino's first game at Anfield, Van Dijk expected fit and a Maguire back check

    Kick-off is 4:30pm UK time (11:30am ET) on Sunday 22 November. Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of United for the first time, Lisandro Martinez is back from a ban and Harry Maguire is being assessed. Virgil van Dijk is expected to be fit for Liverpool.

  • Premier League Lineups November 20, 2026

    Pochettino says he will pick his first Man Utd team “tomorrow”, and Maguire and Dorgu have both trained

    In his first news conference as Manchester United head coach, Mauricio Pochettino confirmed Bruno Fernandes stays captain and said he will decide the squad for Liverpool on Saturday. Harry Maguire and Patrick Dorgu completed full training on Thursday; Luke Shaw is a doubt.

Arsenal team news

  • Bukayo Saka (quad): expected to stay out until December, according to Fantasy Football Scout's early Gameweek 11 round-up.
  • Max Dowman: serves the third game of a three-match ban.
  • Martin Zubimendi (hamstring): the new worry. He came off in Spain's Nations League defeat by England on Sunday, seemingly clutching his hamstring. There was no update on the extent of the problem when the round-up was published. Our Spain vs England team news covers how that game was set up.

For a reminder of how Arsenal lined up the last time they rotated, see our Arsenal lineup against Slavia Prague.

Newcastle team news

  • Joelinton (hamstring): looks set to miss out again. He was still absent when Newcastle trained on Monday.
  • Ewen Jaouen (hamstring): also expected to miss out.
  • Nico Gonzalez and Joe Willock: missed that session through illness only.
  • Jacob Ramsey: available again after serving a one-match suspension.

What to watch for

The biggest call is in Arsenal's midfield. If Zubimendi is not fit, Arsenal would be without him as well as Saka. For Newcastle, the return of Ramsey gives them an extra option while Joelinton is out.
