Wild's 1,000th regular-season win was a 4-1 over the Sharks

By Staff Desk

The Wild beat the Sharks 4-1 on Nov. 6 for their 1,000th regular-season win, in 1,995 games. Wallstedt stopped 23 shots.

Last updated November 20, 2026

Update, Nov. 20, 2026: The headline now follows the NHL.com recap, which calls this the franchise's 1,000th regular-season win, in 1,995 games. The league's X post still says "1,000th win all-time."

The Minnesota Wild beat the San Jose Sharks 4-1 on Friday, Nov. 6, 2026, at Grand Casino Arena in St. Paul. NHL.com's recap, dated Nov. 7, says that win was the fastest any franchise has reached 1,000 regular-season victories, in 1,995 games. Those two figures were checked against the recap on Nov. 20, 2026.

Jesper Wallstedt made 23 saves. Yaroslav Askarov made 25 for San Jose. On this desk a goalie start is confirmed when the league recap says he played. This recap does.

How the 4-1 was scored

Kirill Kaprizov opened the scoring on a 5-on-3 at 7:55 of the first period, his league-leading 16th goal, off a cross-ice pass from Quinn Hughes. Macklin Celebrini tied it at 12:11 of the second, redirecting Dmitry Orlov's shot. Maxim Shabanov put Minnesota back in front at 14:15 on another power play, tapping in a pass from Kaprizov. Hughes made it 3-1 at 6:54 of the third. Zach Bogosian made it 4-1 at 13:58, his first goal of the season.

Kaprizov finished with a goal and three assists. Hughes had a goal and two assists. A later desk story covers Bill Guerin's comment that the Wild could keep Hughes past the trade deadline without an extension.

The recap lists Minnesota at 12-3-3, with three wins in a row and a 6-0-1 mark in the previous seven games. San Jose fell to 7-10-1.

Two wordings of the same night

The league's X account posted that night:

"The @minnesotawild pick up their 1,000th win all-time!"

That post is what this page cited on Nov. 7. The recap's note is narrower: 1,000 regular-season wins, in 1,995 games. This page now leads with the recap. It does not treat the two phrases as the same count.
