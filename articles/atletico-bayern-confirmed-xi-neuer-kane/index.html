Neuer Returns for Bayern at Atlético as Kane Leads Injury-Hit Attack

By Staff Desk

Analysis

Bayern Munich’s confirmed Champions League XI includes Manuel Neuer and Harry Kane against Atlético Madrid, with several attacking absences shaping the selection.

Last updated November 3, 2026

Manuel Neuer is in Bayern Munich’s confirmed starting lineup for Tuesday’s Champions League visit to Atlético Madrid, completing a significant late fitness turnaround. Michael Olise is also named in the team published by Bulinews, while Harry Kane leads the attack. The game is scheduled for 21:00 Central European Time at the Metropolitano on November 3.

Bayern’s published eleven is Neuer; Konrad Laimer, Dayot Upamecano, Jonathan Tah and Josip Stanišić; Joshua Kimmich and Tom Bischof; Lennart Karl, Michael Olise and Alphonso Davies; and Kane. The lineup report prints Olise’s name as “Oluse,” but the accompanying injury coverage identifies the player as Michael Olise. Bayern’s selection gives the team a recognized focal point in Kane and a mixture of experienced defenders and less-established attacking options.

Neuer’s inclusion was far from assured earlier in the week. Bulinews reported that the goalkeeper had returned from foot problems in Bayern’s 2-2 Champions League draw with Arsenal, only to miss the next three matches following a setback. He trained with the squad on Monday, as did Olise, who had been forced off late in Saturday’s 2-2 Bundesliga draw with Borussia Dortmund and practiced with his right knee bandaged.

The fitness news does not amount to a fully restored squad. Jamal Musiala and Luis Díaz were restricted to individual training on Monday, with Díaz returning to the pitch for the first time since his muscle injury. Serge Gnabry and Ismael Saibari were also unavailable after injuries suffered against Dortmund. Those absences explain why the attacking choices around Kane merit as much attention as Neuer’s return.

Atlético’s confirmed eleven is Jan Oblak; Marc Pubill, Cristian Romero, Dávid Hancko and Alejandro Grimaldo; Giuliano Simeone, Marcos Llorente, Pablo Barrios and Álex Baena; Julián Álvarez and Alexander Sørloth. Diego Simeone’s pairing of Álvarez and Sørloth gives Atlético two forwards with contrasting ways of troubling a defense: movement into space and a physical presence in central areas. Bayern’s central defenders will have to coordinate their marking while watching runners from midfield.

The match arrives with both clubs still seeking a stronger position in the league phase. Bulinews reports that Bayern drew its last two European matches, against Viking FK and Arsenal, leaving it with five points from three games. Atlético had four points from three, despite entering the contest in strong domestic form: second in La Liga after a 2-0 victory over Levante on Saturday. Those figures explain why the fixture has consequences beyond the prestige of the two clubs.

Bayern are also operating with an unusual coaching arrangement. Assistant coach Aaron Danks is taking charge again in the absence of Vincent Kompany, according to the confirmed-lineup report. Matchday decisions about substitutions and managing recently injured players therefore fall to a staff working without the usual head coach on the touchline.

Neuer’s return potentially helps Bayern organize its defensive line and build possession from the back. Yet his presence should not be interpreted as proof that the earlier foot issue has no residual risk. Likewise, Olise starting after the knee scare confirms he was judged available, not that the injury was insignificant. Their actual workload and performance remain questions for the match itself.

The key lineup story is the balance between returning experience and missing attacking depth. Bayern have Neuer, Olise and Kane available for a demanding away match, but Musiala, Díaz, Gnabry and Saibari are absent. Atlético have named Álvarez and Sørloth together. The teams are confirmed; predictions about the result are not.
