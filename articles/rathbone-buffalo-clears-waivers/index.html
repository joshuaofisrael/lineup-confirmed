Elliotte Friedman: Jack Rathbone (BUF) clears waivers

By Staff Desk

NHL insider Elliotte Friedman reported that Buffalo Sabres defenceman Jack Rathbone cleared waivers, a roster availability update for Buffalo's blue line.

Last updated November 7, 2026

Elliotte Friedman reported that Jack Rathbone of the Buffalo Sabres cleared waivers.

In a 6 November 2026 post, the Sportsnet insider wrote:

"Cleared

Rathbone (BUF) on waivers"

What clearance means for the roster

A player who clears waivers is available for assignment without another NHL club claiming him on that wire. Friedman's note does not by itself announce a demotion, recall, or trade; it confirms the waiver result. Lineup Confirmed readers should still wait for Buffalo's official transaction or game-day lineup before projecting who dresses next.

Primary source: Friedman's clearance post.
