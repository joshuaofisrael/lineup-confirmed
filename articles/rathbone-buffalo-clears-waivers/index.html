Jack Rathbone cleared Buffalo waivers on Nov. 6, Friedman says

By Staff Desk

Friedman reported that Jack Rathbone cleared waivers for the Sabres on Nov. 6. Sportsnet said Buffalo waived him and did not report an assignment.

Last updated November 20, 2026

Update, Nov. 20, 2026: Sportsnet's Nov. 6 story was checked again. It says the Sabres waived Jack Rathbone. It does not say he cleared, and it does not name an assignment.

In a Nov. 6, 2026 post, the Sportsnet insider wrote:

"Cleared

Rathbone (BUF) on waivers"

What Sportsnet reported the same day

Sportsnet's Nov. 6 story is mostly about James Reimer. In the same piece it says the Sabres waived Rathbone. The details it gives: he was 27, he had not appeared in a game for Buffalo, and his last NHL stint was 11 games with the Vancouver Canucks in 2022-23, with one goal and one assist.

That story does say Reimer cleared and was assigned to the Hartford Wolf Pack. It does not say either of those things for Rathbone. Our Reimer note is the other name on that wire.

What "cleared" does not settle

A clearance means no other NHL club claimed him on that waiver run. It is not, by itself, a demotion, a recall, or a trade. Friedman's post is the clearance. Sportsnet's story, re-read on Nov. 20, stops at the waiver. Neither source says which roster Rathbone dressed on next, so this page does not either.
