Arsenal Confirmed XI vs Slavia Prague: Arteta Makes Six Champions League Changes

By Staff Desk

Analysis

Mikel Arteta named a rotated Champions League XI for Slavia Prague on 4 November 2026—Raya; White, Konsa, Gabriel, Hincapié; Zubimendi, Rice, Merino; Madueke, Eze, Gyökeres—with Saliba among the substitutes after a back injury.

Last updated November 5, 2026

Mikel Arteta made six changes for Arsenal's Champions League visit to Slavia Prague on 4 November 2026, naming a rotated starting eleven that still included all three of the club's World Cup winners. Arsenal's official team news published the line-up ahead of kick-off at the Fortuna Arena. The club had teased the announcement on X with "Guess the player" and "Team news dropping".

Confirmed Arsenal XI

Arsenal's published starting line-up was: Raya; White, Konsa, Gabriel, Hincapié; Zubimendi, Rice, Merino; Madueke, Eze, Gyökeres.

That matches the LINE-UPS block on arsenal.com. The substitutes listed on the same page were Arrizabalaga, Meslier, Timber, Calafiori, Saliba, Mosquera, Lewis-Skelly, Guimarães, Ødegaard, Tzolis, Dowman and Havertz. Evening Standard coverage tracked the same confirmed team news.

In a conventional reading of that eleven, Raya starts in goal; White, Konsa, Gabriel and Hincapié form the back four; Zubimendi, Rice and Merino fill central midfield; Madueke and Eze support Gyökeres in attack. Arsenal's own article does not print formation numbers beyond the line-up string, so shape labels beyond that published order should not be invented.

What changed from Anfield

Arteta's team news explains the six changes from the side that started at Liverpool. Martin Zubimendi and Mikel Merino started in midfield, with Merino returning to a venue where he scored twice in a 3–0 win a year earlier, Arsenal note. Piero Hincapié received his first Champions League start of the season at left-back, while Ben White replaced Jurriën Timber on the right of defence.

Eberechi Eze started with a chance to score in a third European game in a row, and Viktor Gyökeres was preferred to Kai Havertz up front after Havertz's winner against Liverpool three days earlier. Havertz, Martin Ødegaard, Timber, Riccardo Calafiori, Bruno Guimarães and Christos Tzolis dropped to the bench from the Anfield XI.

The rotation therefore touches defence, midfield and attack rather than changing only one department. Keeping Rice and Gabriel in the side preserved a familiar defensive spine while resting other regulars after Premier League duty.

Saliba's return to the matchday squad

William Saliba was named among the substitutes and, Arsenal say, was looking for his first minutes of the campaign after recovering from a back injury. Inclusion on the bench confirms he was judged available for selection; it is not by itself proof of a full ninety minutes or of a completed recovery timeline beyond what the club stated. Having both Arrizabalaga and Meslier among the goalkeeping options on the bench likewise reflects matchday depth listed by the club, not a prediction about who would replace Raya if needed.

How to read the selection

The XI is a classic European rotation after a Premier League fixture: experienced spines (Raya, Gabriel, Rice) mixed with rested or rotated attackers and a first Champions League start of the season for Hincapié. World Cup winners starting, as Arsenal highlighted, underlines that rotation did not mean a youth experiment. The confirmed names are the story; match outcome and individual ratings sit outside this line-up note.
