What an NHL Back-to-Back Changes About the Starting Goalie

By Staff Desk

Analysis

The second night of a back-to-back is its own starter decision. Flyers, Devils and Capitals show when that decision is confirmed and when it is still open.

Last updated November 20, 2026

A back-to-back is two games on consecutive days. The goalie who starts the first game is a fact about that game. The goalie for the second game is a separate fact, and it stays unconfirmed here until a team, the league, or an on-site reporter says who it is. This is how this desk labels starters. It is not a league rule, and it is not a prediction.

This week's slate is a clean set of examples. Two clubs changed goalies on the second night, and both changes are in the game stories. A third club has played the first night and has not named anyone for the second.

When the second starter is in the recap

Flyers. Tuesday's starter was Dan Vladar, as our report from that morning said. On Wednesday, the second night, Joseph Woll started at Washington and made 25 saves in a 5-2 loss, NHL.com's recap says. The Flyers had lost 4-3 in overtime to Columbus the night before. More on Thompson's return is in the Capitals story.

Devils. Connor Hellebuyck started Tuesday at Dallas, his first game for New Jersey, and made 17 saves in a 5-1 win, NHL.com reports. Casey DeSmith made 22 saves for the Stars. On Wednesday in Colorado, Jake Allen started and made 40 saves on 45 shots in a 5-2 loss, per the NHL.com box score. (The NHL.com recap gives 39; we use the box score.) Mackenzie Blackwood made 20 saves in his 300th NHL game, the Avalanche recap says.

"That's a tough back-to-back," Devils coach Sheldon Keefe said after the loss.

Hellebuyck on Tuesday and Allen on Wednesday are both confirmed, because the recaps say they started. The Tuesday plan is in our Hellebuyck debut story.

When the second night was still open

Capitals. Thompson started Wednesday and made 23 saves in the 5-2 win, the status report says. Washington played at Nashville on Thursday at 8 p.m. ET, the NHL schedule feed shows. On Oct. 28 that report did not name a Thursday starter. Wednesday's result did not fill Thursday in.

The box score did, later. Checked Nov. 20, 2026, the NHL box-score feed for game 2026020208 lists Charlie Lindgren as the starter, with 25 saves on 28 shots, in a 4-3 win. Juuse Saros started for Nashville and made 25 saves on 29 shots. Thompson is on the Washington goalie list with no ice time. An earlier line on the Oct. 29 board said Lindgren made 24 saves on 27 shots. That line now matches the feed.

The label on each of those nights is the whole point. Confirmed means a team, the league, or a reporter at the rink said he starts, or the box score shows he did. Expected is a projection, such as a morning-skate crease note, that is not an announcement. Unconfirmed means we do not have that word yet. A back-to-back explains why the question comes up. It does not answer it.

Checked Nov. 20, 2026. The Flyers recap still has Joseph Woll at 25 saves in the 5-2 loss, and Logan Thompson at 23 saves. The Dallas recap still has Connor Hellebuyck at 17 saves and Casey DeSmith at 22 in the 5-1 win. The Avalanche recap still says Jake Allen made 39 saves. The box-score feed for that game still lists Allen as the starter with 40 saves on 45 shots, which is the figure this page uses.

Later notes on the same question: Joseph Woll started for the Flyers at Ottawa on Nov. 19, and Philipp Grubauer was pulled in Calgary's win over Seattle on the first night of a back-to-back. The labels are set out in how a starter gets confirmed. Nothing here is betting advice.
