Flames' Devin Cooley Has Started Three Straight Wins Before Maple Leafs Visit

By Staff Desk

Devin Cooley has started each of Calgary's three straight wins ahead of Monday's game with Toronto. Here is where both creases stand before any starter is announced.

Last updated October 26, 2026

The Toronto Maple Leafs make their only visit of the season to the Scotiabank Saddledome on Monday at 8:30 p.m. ET. As of Monday morning, neither club has named a starter.

Flames: unconfirmed. Devin Cooley has started Calgary's past three games, and the Flames won all three. NHL.com's On Tap gives his numbers over that run as 3-0-0 with a 1.33 goals-against average and a .962 save percentage. TSN notes that the wins came against Carolina, Minnesota and Detroit. In Saturday's 4-1 win over the Red Wings, Cooley stopped 28 of 29 shots, per the Flames' game-day notes. The team has not said whether he gets a fourth straight start or whether Dustin Wolf goes in.

Maple Leafs: unconfirmed. Anthony Stolarz started Saturday's 7-2 win at Edmonton, per the NHL.com box score. TSN's Mark Masters reported that Sergei Bobrovsky was on the ice for Sunday's optional practice. Bobrovsky had not played since Thursday, when he allowed four goals on 25 shots, his lowest save percentage in eight starts this season. Toronto has won four straight, TSN notes, and NHL.com says Auston Matthews enters one point shy of 800 for his career.

Both teams usually reveal their starter after the morning skate or at warmups. Check the Oct. 26 board for updates. Nothing here is betting advice.
