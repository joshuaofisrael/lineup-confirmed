NHL Starting Goalies November 19, 2026

Tocchet sticks with Joseph Woll and saves Vladar for Saturday, so Flyers face old goalie Samuel Ersson in Ottawa

By Staff Desk

Joseph Woll is confirmed in goal for Philadelphia at Ottawa on Thursday, and former Flyer Samuel Ersson is confirmed for the Senators. Dan Vladar is expected to start Saturday.

Last updated November 19, 2026

Both starters are confirmed for Flyers at Senators tonight (7pm ET). "Lineup update for #PHIvsOTT: Joseph Woll gets the start between the pipes," the Philadelphia Flyers posted on X.

Flyers: Joseph Woll (confirmed)

Flyers reporter Charlie O'Connor posted that there are no lineup changes, and that head coach Rick Tocchet "wanted to give Woll another start because he's been playing well (cited his 3 straight wins) and also that Vladar played well vs. MTL last year. So Vladar goes Saturday."

    Nico Daws is in the Jets' starter's net for tonight, and Semyon Varlamov looks set to start for the Islanders

    Nico Daws took the starter's net at Winnipeg's morning skate and is expected to face the New York Islanders on Thursday, 7pm ET. Semyon Varlamov was in the Islanders' starter's crease. Neither team had confirmed by early afternoon.

Daily Faceoff lists Woll at 4-6-2 with a 2.92 goals against average and a .899 save percentage. It says he has won three straight with a 2.30 GAA and .900 save percentage, and saved 16 of 19 shots in his last outing, a 4-3 win over Buffalo.

Senators: Samuel Ersson (confirmed)

"Sam Ersson will start tonight vs PHI," Coming In Hot posted, and Daily Faceoff has him confirmed against his former team. Ersson is 3-3-2 with a 3.11 GAA and .885 save percentage. Daily Faceoff says he has not played since Linus Ullmark returned from a lower-body injury earlier this month, but posted a 1.98 GAA and .925 save percentage over a five-game stretch in which Ottawa took at least a point each night (3-0-2).

Ottawa is 5-0-1 in its past six games, according to Daily Faceoff.
