Hungary 1-1 Northern Ireland: how the teams lined up, Kelly in for the suspended Donley and the subs that changed it

By Staff Desk

Patrick Kelly started up front in place of the suspended Jamie Donley. Josh Magennis came on at half-time and Kieran Morrison and Brodie Spencer soon after, before Justin Devenny's 65th-minute equaliser sealed promotion to League A.

Last updated November 18, 2026

Northern Ireland drew 1-1 with Hungary at the Puskas Arena in Budapest on Tuesday 17 November to finish top of Nations League Group B2 and win promotion to League A, Sky Sports reports. Barnabas Varga put Hungary ahead after 35 minutes and Justin Devenny equalised in the 65th.

Northern Ireland starting XI

In the order listed on Sky Sports' team sheet: Pierce Charles, Terry Devlin, Tom Atcheson, Ruairi McConville, Ciaron Brown, Justin Devenny, Ethan Galbraith, Trai Hume (captain), Shea Charles, Isaac Price, Patrick Kelly.

Kelly started up front in place of the suspended Jamie Donley. Manager Michael O'Neill watched from the stands while serving a touchline ban, and former captain Steven Davis led the side from the touchline.

Northern Ireland substitutions

  • 45 min: Josh Magennis on for Tom Atcheson
  • 56 min: Kieran Morrison and Brodie Spencer on for Patrick Kelly and Terry Devlin
  • 82 min: Smyth on for Isaac Price

Sky says Magennis's physical presence and the extra pace from the changes made Northern Ireland "a much greater threat going forward". Devenny's equaliser came after a long throw from Hume sat up for him to strike into the top corner.

Hungary starting XI

As listed by Sky: Balazs Toth, A Osvath, M Csinger, A Markgraf, Milos Kerkez, A Csongvai, Callum Styles, Bendeguz Bolla, Andras Schafer, Dominik Szoboszlai (captain), Barnabas Varga.

Sky describes Bolla as a late change to the Hungary side. Hungary's changes were Rajmund Toth for Styles (60 min), Urblik and Peto for Csongvai and Bolla (69 min) and Szendrei for Osvath (84 min).

What O'Neill said

"We had to make substitutions; we had to try and get freshness," O'Neill told his post-match press conference. "I felt in the first half we looked a little bit leggy, which is natural, but I just felt we needed that freshness at the right points in the game."
