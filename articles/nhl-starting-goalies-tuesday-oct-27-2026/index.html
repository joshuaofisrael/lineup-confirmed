NHL Starting Goalies October 26, 2026

NHL Starting Goalies Tuesday, Oct. 27, 2026: Hellebuyck Debuts, Adin Hill In for Vegas

By Staff Desk

All 22 Tuesday starters are now reported: 12 confirmed and 10 expected. Connor Hellebuyck debuts for New Jersey, and Adin Hill starts for Vegas after Carter Hart was hurt at the morning skate.

Last updated October 27, 2026

Tuesday, Oct. 27 has 11 games on the NHL schedule. Updated Tuesday evening (London time), after every team's morning skate or game-day update. All 22 starters have now been reported: 12 are confirmed by a coach, a team or a reporter relaying the coach, and 10 are expected based on morning-skate creases or NHL.com's projected lineups. The two big goalie stories are in New Jersey and Vegas: Connor Hellebuyck makes his Devils debut at Dallas, and Adin Hill starts for the Golden Knights after Carter Hart was hurt at the morning skate. Every status and source is on the Oct. 27 board.

Columbus Blue Jackets at Philadelphia Flyers, 7 p.m. ET

Blue Jackets: Cam Talbot, confirmed. Dave Maetzold, relaying coach Rick Bowness's morning update, reported that Talbot gets a second straight start.

Flyers: Dan Vladar, confirmed. Coach Rick Tocchet named Vladar after the morning skate, per PHLY Sports' Charlie O'Connor. Our Vladar story covers how Philadelphia has split its starts before Wednesday's game in Washington.

Boston Bruins at Carolina Hurricanes, 8 p.m. ET

Bruins: Jeremy Swayman, expected. NESN's Adam Pellerin posted "Jeremy Swayman in net." He is listed as expected until the team or coach says it.

Hurricanes: Pyotr Kochetkov, expected. Kochetkov was in the starter's crease at the morning skate with Brandon Bussi as backup, per the Hurricanes' Walt Ruff. The NHL.com status report adds that coach Rod Brind'Amour believes forward Taylor Hall will play.

Los Angeles Kings at Chicago Blackhawks, 8 p.m. ET

Kings: Anton Forsberg, expected. Los Angeles is on the second night of a back-to-back and did not hold a morning skate. NHL.com says Forsberg is expected to start after Darcy Kuemper made 23 saves in Monday's 6-3 win at the Rangers.

Blackhawks: Spencer Knight, expected. NHL.com says Knight is expected to make his seventh straight start, and Charlie Roumeliotis reported he worked in the starter's net at the morning skate.

Montreal Canadiens at St. Louis Blues, 8 p.m. ET

Canadiens: Jacob Fowler, confirmed. The Canadiens posted that Fowler starts. NHL.com's earlier projection had listed Jakub Dobes first; the team's announcement takes precedence.

Blues: Jordan Binnington, confirmed. NHL.com's game-day report says Binnington starts for the first time since Oct. 17. The Post-Dispatch's Matthew DeFranks saw him in the starter's net at the morning skate.

New York Islanders at Nashville Predators, 8 p.m. ET

Islanders: Semyon Varlamov, confirmed. Stefen Rosner reported that Varlamov gets the start. Ilya Sorokin started Sunday at Dallas.

Predators: Juuse Saros, expected. Saros was in the starter's crease at the morning skate, per Rosner.

New Jersey Devils at Dallas Stars, 8 p.m. ET

Devils: Connor Hellebuyck, confirmed. "He is going tonight," coach Sheldon Keefe said, per NHL.com. It is Hellebuyck's first NHL game since April 13. Full story: Hellebuyck starts his Devils debut.

Stars: Casey DeSmith, confirmed. Stars radio analyst Bruce LeVine reported that DeSmith starts.

Florida Panthers at Winnipeg Jets, 8 p.m. ET

Panthers: Akira Schmid, confirmed. The Panthers posted that Schmid starts, per coach Paul Maurice.

Jets: Clay Stevenson, expected. Stevenson took warm-up shots in the starter's net, and Jets broadcaster Mitchell Clinton said it looks like he gets the call; CJOB's Kelly Moore also pointed to Stevenson. NHL.com's projected lineup lists Stuart Skinner first, so the reports conflict, and we keep Stevenson at expected until Winnipeg confirms.

Minnesota Wild at Utah Mammoth, 9 p.m. ET

Wild: Jesper Wallstedt, confirmed. Wild radio's Joe O'Donnell reported that coach John Hynes confirmed Wallstedt starts.

Mammoth: Karel Vejmelka, expected. Vejmelka was first off the ice at the morning skate, KSL Sports reported, and NHL.com's projected lineup lists him first.

Ottawa Senators at Vegas Golden Knights, 10 p.m. ET

Senators: Jonas Johansson, confirmed. Coach Travis Green kept the same lineup, with Johansson starting again, Jackson Starr reported.

Golden Knights: Adin Hill, confirmed. Carter Hart was injured during the morning skate and is being evaluated, and Hill will start, NHL.com's status report says. More in our Adin Hill story.

Vancouver Canucks at Anaheim Ducks, 10 p.m. ET

Canucks: Leevi Meriläinen, expected. Vancouver did not hold a morning skate. NHL.com's projected lineup lists Meriläinen first, with Kevin Lankinen as backup.

Ducks: Lukas Dostal, confirmed. Coach Joel Quenneville said Dostal starts, Derek Lee reported.

Buffalo Sabres at San Jose Sharks, 10:30 p.m. ET

Sabres: Colten Ellis, expected. NHL.com's projected lineup lists Ellis first, with Ukko-Pekka Luukkonen as backup.

Sharks: Yaroslav Askarov, expected. The same NHL.com projection lists Askarov first, with Alex Nedeljkovic as backup.

Results go on the Oct. 27 board once games finish. Next up: the Wednesday board and the Thursday board. For Monday's results, see our Rangers story and Red Wings story. Nothing here is betting advice.
