Brossoit improves to 4-0-0 since recall as Gauthier's hat trick sinks Penguins

By Staff Desk

Laurent Brossoit stopped 32 shots in Anaheim's 5-2 win over Pittsburgh on Sunday. Arturs Silovs made 20 saves, and Cutter Gauthier scored his third hat trick in five games.

Last updated November 9, 2026

Laurent Brossoit made 32 saves, and Cutter Gauthier scored his third hat trick in five games, as the Anaheim Ducks beat the Pittsburgh Penguins 5-2 at Honda Center on Sunday, NHL.com reported. Brossoit is 4-0-0 since Anaheim recalled him from San Diego of the AHL on October 31.

The pregame preview listed Ville Husso as a possible Ducks starter after he relieved Lukas Dostal in Calgary on Friday and stopped all 10 shots he faced. Brossoit got the start instead. Dostal was listed among Anaheim's scratches. For Pittsburgh, Arturs Silovs was expected to start after Sergei Murashov made 26 saves in Saturday's 5-2 win at Los Angeles, and he did.

Goalie results

Team Goalie Saves Result
Ducks Laurent Brossoit 32 W
Penguins Arturs Silovs 20 L

"He's been a rock for us this little win streak we've had so far," Gauthier said of Brossoit.

How it went

  • Mikael Granlund opened the scoring on the power play at 8:26 of the first period, with one second left on the man advantage.
  • Gauthier scored at 6:28 and 9:11 of the second period, the first a no-look shot between Silovs' pads.
  • Tristan Luneau made it 4-0 on the power play at 5:12 of the third.
  • Kris Letang (8:05) and Rickard Rakell (13:42) scored on the power play with Silovs pulled for an extra attacker.
  • Gauthier completed the hat trick with an empty-net goal at 19:26.

The Penguins could not score during a 1:37 two-man advantage early in the second period.

Streaks and records

  • Gauthier has goals in six straight games (13 goals in that span) and 17 points in an eight-game point streak. He is the first player in NHL history to score three hat tricks within the first 10 days of a calendar month.
  • Jackson LaCombe had three assists, extending his assist streak to 10 games and tying the Ducks record held by Ryan Getzlaf and Corey Perry.
  • Anaheim (10-7-1) has won five in a row and has scored a power-play goal in eight straight games.
  • Pittsburgh (6-10-2) lost the night after a win in Los Angeles had snapped a four-game losing streak.

Injuries listed before the game

  • Ducks: A.J. Greer (upper body), Ian Moore (lower body), Ryan Poehling (MCL) and Troy Terry (hip) were listed as injured. Frank Vatrano was a scratch.
