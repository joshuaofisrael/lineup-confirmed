Germany vs Netherlands team news: Schlotterbeck set to return, De Jong out for Monday's group decider

By Staff Desk

Klopp's Germany need a win in Berlin on Monday (20:45 CET) to guarantee a quarter-final place. Frenkie de Jong misses out for the Netherlands, with Teun Koopmeiners likely to step in.

Last updated November 15, 2026

Germany host the Netherlands at Berlin's Olympiastadion on Monday 16 November, kick-off 20:45 CET (2:45pm ET), the DFB says. It is the final Group A2 game, and the stakes are high for Jürgen Klopp's side.

What is at stake

The Netherlands lead the group on 11 points after beating Greece 7-0 on Friday. Germany and Greece have eight each after Germany won 3-1 in Serbia, Yahoo Sports reports. A German win guarantees a quarter-final place in March 2027. A defeat would leave Germany third and could send them into a League A/B relegation play-off.

Germany team news

Klopp surprisingly left Nico Schlotterbeck out against Serbia in favour of Malick Thiaw to deal with long balls. Against a Dutch side expected to keep the ball on the ground, Schlotterbeck is likely to return, Yahoo Sports says. Nathaniel Brown could replace Maximilian Mittelstädt at left-back. Niclas Füllkrug, the Bundesliga's top scorer with nine goals, is back in the 24-man squad, according to the DFB.

Netherlands team news

Frenkie de Jong is set to miss the game with a knock. Teun Koopmeiners is likely to partner Joey Veerman in central midfield, according to Yahoo Sports, and head coach Xavi Hernández has little reason to change much else after the win over Greece.

Full predicted lineups were not yet available from our sources. We will update when the teams are confirmed.
