Liverpool vs Man Utd team news: Pochettino's first game at Anfield, Van Dijk expected fit and a Maguire back check

By Staff Desk

Kick-off is 4:30pm UK time (11:30am ET) on Sunday 22 November. Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of United for the first time, Lisandro Martinez is back from a ban and Harry Maguire is being assessed. Virgil van Dijk is expected to be fit for Liverpool.

Last updated November 19, 2026

Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of Manchester United for the first time at Anfield on Sunday 22 November. Kick-off is 4:30pm UK time (11:30am ET), live on Sky Sports, Sky Sports reports.

United are in the relegation zone. Michael Carrick was sacked after the 4-4 draw with Aston Villa before the international break, and United's only league win this season was the 5-2 victory over Ipswich in August, according to Sky. Liverpool are fourth on 18 points, nine behind leaders Arsenal, after beating Crystal Palace 3-1 in their last match.

This is early team news, compiled before the pre-match press conferences. We will publish the confirmed lineups when the teams are announced.

Liverpool team news

  • Virgil van Dijk: withdrew from Netherlands duty with a niggle but is expected to be fit for Sunday, Sky says. Fantasy Football Scout notes Liverpool were still waiting for Andoni Iraola's update.
  • Ryan Gravenberch (heel): missed the matches before the break. Sky says the injury needs to be managed, and Fantasy Football Scout says Iraola linked his involvement to pain management.
  • Jeremie Frimpong (hamstring): back in training.
  • Conor Bradley and Giovanni Leoni (knee): back in training after long lay-offs but not in contention this weekend.
  • Hugo Ekitike (Achilles): still out.
  • Dominik Szoboszlai: played 90 minutes twice for Hungary despite knee pain.

Man Utd team news

  • Carlos Baleba, Benjamin Sesko and Manuel Ugarte: long-term absentees, still out.
  • Lisandro Martinez: available again after suspension.
  • Harry Maguire: being assessed after a back problem in the Villa draw. Fantasy Football Scout describes it as a back spasm and says he is expected to recover.
  • Luke Shaw and Marcus Rashford: being monitored and could be close to a return.
  • Patrick Dorgu: came home early from the Denmark squad for "recovery time after his injury".
