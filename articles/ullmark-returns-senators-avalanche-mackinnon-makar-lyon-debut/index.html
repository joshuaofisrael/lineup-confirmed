NHL Starting Goalies November 13, 2026

Ullmark makes 26 saves in return as Senators beat Avalanche in OT; MacKinnon and Makar leave injured

By Staff Desk

Linus Ullmark returned from a nine-game absence to help Ottawa beat Colorado 4-3 in overtime. Nathan MacKinnon and Cale Makar left with lower-body injuries, and Alex Lyon made his season debut for Buffalo.

Last updated November 13, 2026

Linus Ullmark was back in goal for the Ottawa Senators on Thursday and made 26 saves in a 4-3 overtime win against the Colorado Avalanche at Canadian Tire Centre, NHL.com reported. Shane Pinto scored twice, including the winner at 2:08 of overtime.

Goalie results: Thursday, 12 November

  • Senators: Linus Ullmark, started, 26 saves, win (OT). It was his first game after missing nine with a lower-body strain.
  • Avalanche: Scott Wedgewood, in goal, 22 saves, overtime loss.
  • Sabres: Alex Lyon made his season debut at the Chicago Blackhawks, after being activated off injured reserve, per the NHL Status Report.

Ullmark's return

Ullmark had been out since leaving a 5-1 win over the Florida Panthers on 21 October. Before the injury he was 7-1-0 with a 1.33 goals-against average and a .945 save percentage in eight starts, the Status Report says. Ottawa (12-6-2) is 3-0-1 in its past four games.

Avalanche injuries

Nathan MacKinnon and Cale Makar both left in the second period with lower-body injuries. MacKinnon went off after trying to reverse hit Artem Zub, and Makar, who had left practice early on Sunday, played one more brief shift before staying on the bench. Coach Jared Bednar had no update on either player after the game, NHL.com reported.

Lyon back for Buffalo

Lyon had been out since an upper-body injury in preseason. Coach Lindy Ruff said the Sabres had given him extra time and that he had "been ready now for a while." Buffalo placed forward Konsta Helenius on injured reserve with an upper-body injury. Lyon went 20-10-4 with a 2.77 GAA and .907 save percentage in 36 games last season.

Colorado had not given an update on MacKinnon or Makar when we checked on Friday morning.
