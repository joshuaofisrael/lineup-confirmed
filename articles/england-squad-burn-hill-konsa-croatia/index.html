England team news: Burn in for Guehi, Hill gets first call up and Konsa out of Croatia game

By Staff Desk

England have added Dan Burn and uncapped James Hill to Thomas Tuchel's squad, while Ezri Konsa will not be in contention against Croatia at Wembley on Thursday.

Last updated November 10, 2026

England's defensive options for the final two UEFA Nations League group games have changed twice in two days. Newcastle's Dan Burn and Bournemouth's James Hill have joined Thomas Tuchel's squad, and Ezri Konsa will not be in contention for Thursday's match against Croatia, according to the FA's squad updates page.

Burn replaces Guehi

On Monday the FA said Burn had been added to the squad after Marc Guehi withdrew through injury. Guehi was substituted at half time of Manchester City's match at Nottingham Forest on Saturday and will remain with his club. Burn, who has 11 caps, joined the rest of the group at Tottenham Hotspur's training ground on Monday.

Hill's first senior call up

On Tuesday morning the FA confirmed that Hill, 24, has received his first call up to the senior squad. The Bournemouth defender has represented England at under 20 and under 21 level.

Konsa to miss Croatia

The same update said Konsa will not be in contention for Croatia at Wembley on Thursday as he continues to follow his own programme. The FA expects him to return to full training later this week, before Sunday's trip to Spain.

The fixtures

England host Croatia at Wembley on Thursday, November 12, at 7.45pm UK time, then play Spain in Madrid on Sunday, November 15, also at 7.45pm UK time, according to the original squad announcement. England are second in Group C and will secure a quarter final place for next March if they avoid defeat against Croatia. To finish top, they would need to win both games and have a better goal difference than Spain.

Defenders now in the squad

With Guehi out and Burn and Hill in, the defenders named are Trent Alexander-Arnold, Jarrad Branthwaite, Dan Burn, Lewis Hall, James Hill, Ezri Konsa, Jarell Quansah, Djed Spence and Ben White.

What this means for the lineup

None of these updates confirm a starting XI. England normally name the team about an hour before kick off, and Lineup Confirmed will publish the confirmed lineup for Croatia once the FA releases it.
