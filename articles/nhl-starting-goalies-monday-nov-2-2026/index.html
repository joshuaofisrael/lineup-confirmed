NHL Starting Goalies for Monday, Nov. 2: Vasilevskiy, DiPietro and Annunen Projected on a Light Three-Game Slate

By Staff Desk

Monday's NHL slate has just three games: Lightning at Red Wings, Blackhawks at Predators and Bruins at Blues. NHL.com projects Vasilevskiy, Gibson, Knight, Annunen, DiPietro and Hofer, none of it confirmed yet.

Last updated November 2, 2026

Short answer: nobody is confirmed yet. Monday, Nov. 2 is a light night on the NHL schedule, with only three games, and NHL.com's projected lineups have Andrei Vasilevskiy, John Gibson, Spencer Knight, Justus Annunen, Michael DiPietro and Joel Hofer all listed first in goal. None of the six has been named a starter by a team, the league or a reporter at the rink, so every one of them is expected, not confirmed. Daylight saving time ended Sunday, so all times below are Eastern Standard Time.

Game (ET) Away, last start Home, last start
Lightning at Red Wings, 7 p.m. Andrei Vasilevskiy, 29 saves through overtime in a 5-4 shootout loss to Nashville (Oct. 31) John Gibson, 10 saves on 15 shots in a 5-4 loss to St. Louis (Oct. 31)
Blackhawks at Predators, 8 p.m. Arvid Soderblom, 27 saves on 32 shots in a 5-4 overtime loss to Boston (Oct. 31) Justus Annunen, 22 saves through overtime in a 5-4 shootout win over Tampa Bay (Oct. 31)
Bruins at Blues, 8 p.m. Michael DiPietro, 37 saves on 41 shots in a 5-4 overtime win over Chicago (Oct. 31) Joel Hofer, 24 saves on 28 shots in a 5-4 win over Detroit (Oct. 31)

Lightning at Red Wings, 7 p.m. ET

Lightning: Andrei Vasilevskiy, expected. NHL.com's projected lineups list him first in goal for Monday. He has not missed a start since Oct. 29 and made 29 saves through overtime in Saturday's 5-4 shootout loss to Nashville.

Red Wings: John Gibson, expected. Also listed first in NHL.com's projection. Gibson made just 10 saves on 15 shots in Saturday's 5-4 loss to St. Louis.

Blackhawks at Predators, 8 p.m. ET

Blackhawks: Spencer Knight, expected. NHL.com's projection lists Knight first, with Arvid Soderblom second. That would be a rotation back to Knight, who has not started since Thursday, Oct. 29. Soderblom made 27 saves on 32 shots in Saturday's overtime loss at Boston. It is a projection, not an announcement, so treat it as unconfirmed until Chicago's morning skate.

Predators: Justus Annunen, expected. Listed first after his 22-save overtime shootout win over Tampa Bay on Saturday.

Bruins at Blues, 8 p.m. ET

Bruins: Michael DiPietro, expected. DiPietro made 37 saves on 41 shots to win in overtime against Chicago on Saturday, and NHL.com's projected lineups list him first again for Monday.

Blues: Joel Hofer, expected. Listed first, with Jordan Binnington second, after Hofer made 24 saves on 28 shots in Saturday's win over Detroit.
