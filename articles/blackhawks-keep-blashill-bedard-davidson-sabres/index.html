Blackhawks keep Blashill as coach; Bedard says players have '100 percent belief'

By Staff Desk

GM Kyle Davidson said he has full confidence in coach Jeff Blashill despite a 4-13-1 start. Bowen Byram and Patrick Kane practiced Wednesday before Thursday's home game against Buffalo.

Last updated November 12, 2026

Jeff Blashill will stay as coach of the Chicago Blackhawks, general manager Kyle Davidson said Wednesday, and captain Connor Bedard said the players have "100 percent belief" in him, NHL.com reported.

Chicago is 4-13-1 and has lost six in a row (0-5-1) before hosting the Buffalo Sabres on Thursday (8 p.m. ET). The last of those was a 9-2 loss at Carolina on Saturday, followed by four days without a game.

What Davidson said

Davidson said he has full confidence in Blashill and took responsibility for the start, the Associated Press reported via TSN. He said former NHL defenseman Mark Eaton, the team's assistant general manager for player development, will join the coaching staff from Monday to work on individual defensive skills.

The Blackhawks allow 4.22 goals per game, second most in the NHL, per NHL.com. Davidson said he is talking with other GMs about trades but will not make a move just to make one, according to the AP.

Lineup watch: Byram and Kane

  • Bowen Byram (upper body): has missed 11 straight games and has seen a specialist. He practiced Wednesday.
  • Patrick Kane (upper body): out since Nov. 2. He also practiced Wednesday.

The AP said both practiced in non-contact jerseys and are on injured reserve, and NHL.com said Blashill did not know when they would return. Our read: both look doubtful for Thursday unless the team says otherwise. NHL.com noted the Blackhawks have not had their full lineup in a game this season.

Blashill, in his second season, is 33-52-15 in 100 games with Chicago, per the AP.
