NHL Starting Goalies Wednesday, Oct. 28: Logan Thompson Back at Practice; Devils on a Back-to-Back

By Staff Desk

Wednesday has four NHL games and no announced starters yet. Capitals goalie Logan Thompson returned to practice Monday, and New Jersey plays the second half of a back-to-back at Colorado.

Last updated October 26, 2026

NHL.com's schedule has four games on Wednesday, Oct. 28. No team has named a starter yet. Below is the latest goalie news for each team and who started its most recent game, from official box scores. We will update the Wednesday board as starters are reported.

Flyers at Capitals, 7:30 p.m. ET

Capitals: unconfirmed. Logan Thompson (lower body) took part in the first 40 minutes of Monday's practice, NHL.com's status report says. Coach Spencer Carbery called it a good step but would not say whether Thompson will be ready Wednesday. Charlie Lindgren started Saturday's overtime win against Buffalo, per the box score.

Flyers: unconfirmed. Philadelphia hosts Columbus on Tuesday, so Wednesday is the second night of a back-to-back. Joseph Woll started Saturday against Anaheim (box score).

Flames at Oilers, 8:30 p.m. ET

Both teams play Monday night. Devin Cooley starts for Calgary against Toronto, and Tristan Jarry starts for Edmonton against Detroit (see our Monday board). Neither club has said who starts the Battle of Alberta. Edmonton is carrying Jarry and Matt Tomkins while Devon Levi is on injured reserve.

Devils at Avalanche, 9 p.m. ET

Devils: unconfirmed. New Jersey plays at Dallas on Tuesday, so this is a back-to-back. Connor Hellebuyck practiced with the Devils for the first time Monday, and coach Sheldon Keefe said the plan is to get him involved on this road trip one day at a time, NHL.com reports. Our Hellebuyck story has more.

Avalanche: unconfirmed. Scott Wedgewood started Sunday's 6-2 loss at Nashville (box score).

Maple Leafs at Kraken, 10 p.m. ET

Maple Leafs: unconfirmed. Sergei Bobrovsky starts Monday at Calgary after Anthony Stolarz played Saturday at Edmonton.

Kraken: unconfirmed. Joey Daccord started Saturday's 5-1 loss to Minnesota (box score).

The Tuesday board covers the 11 games before this slate. Nothing here is betting advice.
