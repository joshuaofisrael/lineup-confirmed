NHL Starting Goalies November 9, 2026

Devils will not start Hellebuyck in his Winnipeg return; Jake Allen expected in goal

By Staff Desk

GM Sunny Mehta said Connor Hellebuyck will not play against the Jets on Tuesday, citing workload. Jake Allen is expected to start, and Hellebuyck is lined up for both Capitals games.

Last updated November 11, 2026

Connor Hellebuyck will not start for the New Jersey Devils when they visit his former team, the Winnipeg Jets, at Canada Life Centre on Tuesday, NHL.com reported. The Devils are expected to start Jake Allen.

General manager Sunny Mehta announced the decision in a statement after Hellebuyck made 23 saves in a 4-0 win against the Toronto Maple Leafs on Sunday. It was his first shutout since New Jersey acquired him from Winnipeg on October 23.

Goalie status for Devils at Jets (Tuesday)

  • Expected starter: Jake Allen
  • Not starting: Connor Hellebuyck (organizational decision, workload management)

Why the Devils are sitting him

"This has been an organizational decision for us that we've debated at length," Mehta said. "Connor Hellebuyck was adamant that he felt a duty to play on Tuesday in Winnipeg. However, in our estimation, playing in this game would potentially give us a reduction in our expected win probability."

Coach Sheldon Keefe said the team is managing Hellebuyck's workload after he skipped training camp and the preseason. Sunday's game was his third start in six nights, and the Devils did not want him to make four starts in eight nights, especially with plans to start him in both games against the Washington Capitals later this week. New Jersey hosts Washington on Thursday and plays in Washington on Saturday.

"This is a goaltender that's not going to play every game of the season. You're always looking for times to give him a night off," Keefe said. "The schedule falling the way that it does it just made sense."

Hellebuyck: "out of my hands"

Hellebuyck said he was "disappointed" but understood. "I was adamant that I want to play, but it is what it is," he said. He had been looking forward to "playing there again, just facing the music, seeing the reaction. It'd be nice, but it's out of my hands."

Hellebuyck is 5-0-0 with a 1.96 goals-against average and a .913 save percentage with New Jersey. In 11 seasons with Winnipeg, he won the Vezina Trophy three times and the Hart Trophy once. He asked for a trade with five years left on a seven-year, $59.5 million contract, was suspended by the Jets on September 16 for not reporting to training camp, and was traded on the day the suspension was due to end.

At his request, the Devils will arrange a media availability after Tuesday's morning skate. Hellebuyck said he will use it to explain why he wanted out of Winnipeg.

Ahead of that same Devils-Jets game, Winnipeg activated Josh Morrissey, who was set to return Tuesday.
