NHL Starting Goalies November 19, 2026

Arturs Silovs gets the Penguins net again despite a .840 slump, and Joel Hofer starts for the Blues

By Staff Desk

Dan Muse confirmed Arturs Silovs starts for Pittsburgh against St. Louis on Thursday, and Joel Hofer is confirmed for the Blues. Daily Faceoff says Silovs is 0-3-1 with a .840 save percentage in his last four games.

Last updated November 19, 2026

Both starters are confirmed for Blues at Penguins tonight (7pm ET). "Dan Muse said Arturs Silovs will start in goal tonight," Penguins broadcaster Dan Potash posted on X, and Pens Inside Scoop posted the same.

Penguins: Arturs Silovs (confirmed)

Daily Faceoff lists Silovs at 5-6-1 with a 3.35 goals against average and a .873 save percentage. He has matched a career high with two shutouts in 12 games, but Daily Faceoff says he has a 4.30 GAA and .840 save percentage in his last four (0-3-1), and has allowed four or more goals in seven of his 12 games. He allowed four on 24 shots in a 5-2 loss to Anaheim last time out.

Pittsburgh has not played since Saturday's 4-1 loss to Ottawa.

Blues: Joel Hofer (confirmed)

"Joel Hofer (STL) will start Thursday @ PIT," Daily Faceoff Fantasy posted, citing Lou Korac. Hofer is 8-6-1 with a 2.27 GAA, a .921 save percentage and two shutouts, according to Daily Faceoff. He allowed four goals on 28 shots in a 4-1 loss to Florida last time, after a six-game stretch with a 1.82 GAA and .938 save percentage.

St. Louis comes in off an 8-6 win over Anaheim.
