Republic of Ireland vs Kosovo team news: Collins out, Dunne in as Hallgrimsson needs a win

By Staff Desk

Kick-off at the Aviva Stadium is 7:45pm Irish time (2:45pm ET). Captain Nathan Collins has returned to Brentford after picking up a knee injury in Linz, and Jimmy Dunne takes his place in the 23-man matchday squad.

Last updated November 17, 2026

The Republic of Ireland host Kosovo tonight, Tuesday 17 November, in their final Nations League group match. Kick-off at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin is 7:45pm Irish time (2:45pm ET), according to the FAI, and the match is live on RTE 2 and RTE Player.

What is at stake

The FAI says the equation is simple: a win guarantees second place, a play-off for promotion to League A and a place in Pot 2 for the Euro 2028 qualifying draw in Belfast on 6 December. Head coach Heimir Hallgrimsson said "a draw will do nothing for us".

Ireland team news

Captain Nathan Collins has gone back to Brentford for assessment and rehabilitation after suffering an injury in Saturday's 1-0 defeat in Austria. RTE Sport reports it is a knee injury. Jimmy Dunne of QPR takes his place in the 23-man matchday squad, and RTE says Hallgrimsson chose not to use Seamus Coleman in a playing capacity. James Abankwah, who had cramp late in Linz, has fully recovered, the FAI says.

Who could replace Collins

The FAI's squad list names eight defenders: Dara O'Shea, John Egan, Liam Kitching, Dunne, Jake O'Brien, Abankwah, Ryan Manning and Liam Scales. Neither source gives a confirmed starting XI, so the back line is the main selection question. Up front, Troy Parrott has scored 10 goals in his last eight competitive internationals, according to the FAI.

Kosovo

Kosovo won the reverse fixture 1-0 earlier this year, the only previous meeting between the sides. Their last three matches have all been draws, the FAI notes. Neither preview lists Kosovo absentees.
